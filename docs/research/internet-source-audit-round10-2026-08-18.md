# Internet source audit — round 10 — 2026-08-18

## Scope

Round 10 targets unusually high-value historical statements that constrain the still-unknown conditioning / top-stage / environmental-coupling mechanism. The search deliberately prioritizes statements that are either:

- close to an inspected or partially dismantled machine;
- unusually specific about tuning, resonance, magnetization, reduced pressure, or hidden structure;
- capable of falsifying a popular reconstruction;
- or directly relevant to the unknown bulk-energy port.

No statement in this round is promoted beyond its transmission chain. In particular, resonance is not treated as an energy source, and large/workshop-machine evidence is not imported into the Marinov M2 historical baseline.

## 1. Sauder / Snicker relay: top element allegedly tunable after partial disassembly

Public preservation page:

- `https://www.robkalmeijer.nl/techniek/experiments/testakica/index.html`

The page contains an anonymized correspondence extract. Its heading says `Correspondence received March 1998`, while the embedded letter is dated `March 10 99`; the date metadata is therefore internally inconsistent and must be preserved as a conflict.

The anonymous letter writer says he spoke with two engineers named **Sauder and Snicker**, who had made measurements on earlier M-L converters. According to that relay:

- they considered many photo/video-based copies to be on the wrong track;
- the converters they had tested were described as extremely simple;
- earlier large jars / capacitive pots were said to be plastic containers filled with aluminium lathe shavings;
- the top component was said **not** to be a conventional `rectifier diode`, but a solenoid-like element with a **movable jumper / contact**, compared with a potentiometer or variable inductor;
- after Sauder and Snicker had **partially disassembled and inspected one machine**, Baumann allegedly had to **tune the movable jumper again**.

The same letter writer explicitly complains that even these engineers did not observe and remember everything precisely. That self-warning materially lowers confidence.

### Evidence classification

`Sauder/Snicker → anonymous letter writer → later web preservation` = **H2 / hearsay relay**.

Machine identity is unresolved. There is no original Sauder/Snicker report, no measurement log, no dismantling photographs, no component value, and no direct Baumann statement currently recovered.

### Engineering consequence

This is nevertheless an unusually valuable acquisition lead because `retuning after partial disassembly` is what one would expect if the top subsystem depended on a sensitive impedance / phase / resonance setting. It justifies an explicit **tunable-L / tunable-impedance research variant**, but not a historical M2 modification.

The aluminium-shavings-pot claim directly conflicts with Marinov's stronger small-machine description (`cylindrical grid + plastic insulation + central copper spiral`). Therefore it is retained only as an **earlier/unspecified-machine H2 variant**.

## 2. L. L., Rorschach, 17-Mar-1984: explicit horseshoe-magnet + electrical-resonance wording

Hans A. Nieper's 1985 book reproduces a witness report signed only `L. L., Rorschach, Switzerland`, titled `Inspection of the Swiss Tachyon Converter on 17 March 1984`.

Public book mirror:

- `https://www.scribd.com/document/131614540/33883875-Nieper-Conversion-of-Gravity-Field-Energy-Revolution-in-Technology-Medicine-and-Society-1985`

A second book reproduction preserves substantially the same passage in `The Secret of the Creative Vacuum — man and the Energy Dance`.

The report already supplies the known M5a geometry: twin ~45-cm acrylic discs, ~50 segment/grid positions, ~60 rpm and separately adjustable lattice pickups. The high-value wording not previously captured explicitly in the repository is that:

- the ~60-rpm speed is described as synchronized by **magnetic impulses**;
- and, according to the report's understanding, the secret of charging the two discs lies in **strong horseshoe magnets that are parts of electrical resonance circuits**.

### Evidence classification

This is **W1 / direct-witness publication reproduced by Nieper**, but the causal sentence is **I1 / witness interpretation** unless an original note or Baumann quotation proves otherwise.

It is not evidence for M2. It concerns the ~1-m-wide / twin-45-cm-disc witness machine provisionally kept as M5a.

### Engineering consequence

This is important because it historicizes a `magnet + electrical resonance circuit + disc charging` interpretation in a **1984 witness report**, earlier than Potter's later back-engineering. It therefore raises the priority of a large-machine test family:

`electrostatic disc/grid frontend → magnetically biased/timed resonant impedance stage → charge routing/storage`.

It does **not** show energy creation by resonance or magnets, and it does not override Marinov's direct small-machine statement rejecting Tesla coils / AC for the M2-family machine he described.

## 3. Hauser expanded report: the wooden base may not have been electrically inert

The same Rob-Kalmeijer preservation page carries an expanded Albert Hauser report of his 14-Feb-1986 visit. Hauser says the shown large machine had a wooden foundation plate. Later in the report he adds, explicitly **according to reports from other visitors**, that the thick wooden foundation plate was thought to alternate layers of **perforated plates and insulating plates**.

### Evidence classification

The visible wooden base = Hauser visit observation.

The alleged internal alternating perforated/insulating stack = **H2 via Hauser's relay of other visitors**, not direct Hauser inspection.

### Engineering consequence

This is a high-value hidden-structure lead because such a stack would make the base a distributed capacitive / field-forming element rather than a mechanically inert board. It also provides a concrete reason to keep `base/environment coupling` as a separate experimental port.

No M2 transfer is permitted. Marinov's M2 rear-metal-plate effect remains the stronger small-machine environmental clue, but this large-machine hearsay provides an independent architectural reason to instrument the base rather than assume it is electrically dead.

## 4. Cathomen workshop transcript: the pickups themselves are described as magnetized

Public German transcript mirror:

- `https://www.allmystery.de/themen/gw11473-5`

In the 2001 workshop sequence, after the upper strengthening/storage discussion, the visitor points to the non-contact pickup structures. Cathomen identifies them as `Abnehmer`. The visitor asks whether they are also magnetized; Cathomen answers **yes**.

### Evidence classification

`Cathomen direct workshop transcript` = **O1/P1 for the workshop machine being discussed**, subject to audio/transcript uncertainty and strict object/timestamp matching.

### Engineering consequence

This is stronger than merely saying the segments are magnetizable. It supports a workshop-machine hypothesis in which the stationary pickup/grid structures have a dual role:

- electrostatic field / capacitive pickup;
- magnetic bias / spatial phase structure.

It still does not prove that the magnets provide net energy, and it must not be silently transferred to M2.

## 5. Cathomen workshop transcript: front component is not a full vacuum and is tied to a condenser

In the same transcript, the visitor asks about a front component and proposes `Funkenstrecke` and then `Vakuum`. Cathomen partly agrees with the spark-gap framing but refuses to explain the part. The crucial operator wording is that **`Vakuum ist es noch nicht`**; after the visitor reformulates it as `luftleer`, Cathomen says the part **is connected with a condenser**.

### Evidence classification

The words `Funkenstrecke`, `Vakuum` and `luftleer` are substantially **interviewer-prompted terminology**. Cathomen's stronger direct information is:

- it is not a full vacuum;
- it is associated with a condenser;
- he deliberately withholds the detailed function.

### Engineering consequence

Model it, if tested, as a **reduced-pressure / gas-discharge / nonlinear switching candidate**, not automatically as a thermionic vacuum tube and not as a `quantum vacuum` energy source.

This also weakens any universal claim that the secret Testatika part was necessarily a sealed evacuated rectifier tube.

## 6. Potter vacuum-valve identification remains a reconstruction, not recovered hardware

Paul E. Potter's back-engineered Testatika text interprets the horizontal glass top tube as a homemade thermionic vacuum rectifier and develops a detailed anode/grid/cathode construction from photographs.

Public preservation:

- `https://www.rexresearch.com/testatik/testart.htm`

That interpretation remains useful as a reversible engineering hypothesis, but it is not an observed dismantled component. It also sits in tension with closer source lines:

- Hauser's expanded report says, as his opinion, the system was not evacuated because a smaller machine had a quite open rectifier;
- Cathomen says the discussed front component is `not yet` a vacuum.

These may describe different components and variants, so there is no direct one-to-one contradiction. The correct conclusion is narrower: **a universal vacuum-tube identification is not source-secure**.

## 7. Environmental-source line: preserve the strong clues but do not promote ordinary air ions to a 100-W source

Round 3 already recorded Albert Hauser's later retrospective/operator line:

- the machine's physical location was changed to test a hidden-transmitter/focus idea;
- Baumann reportedly said it would not work in space because it collected/sorted atmospheric charged ions;
- doors/windows closed reportedly caused stopping; opening a window allowed restart;

Round 10 does not upgrade these claims. It does, however, connect them to two newly emphasized structural clues:

- a potentially electrically active layered base (H2, large machine);
- the M2 rear-metal-plate stop (direct Marinov observation, already canonical).

The correct research model therefore keeps **environment / base / rear-field coupling as an explicit port**. Conventional fair-weather atmospheric ion current remains far too small to explain ~100 W by itself; the source question remains open.

## 8. Dry versus cold air — correction

The historical source base supports **dry air / low humidity** as favorable. Marinov reports fewer startup pushes in dry air and more under humid conditions; the institutional Methernitha technical description also emphasizes humidity.

This crawl did **not** recover a reliable historical source establishing that **cold temperature itself** is a required or optimal condition.

Therefore:

- `dry / low RH` = source-supported operating variable;
- `cold` = currently **NOT SOURCE-VERIFIED** as an independent Testatika requirement;
- temperature remains an experimental variable because it affects absolute humidity, leakage, gas density and discharge thresholds.

## 10. Cross-source convergence — what genuinely became stronger

Without collapsing machine variants, the following pattern now has several differently transmitted supports:

1. **L. L. 1984 / M5a:** magnetic impulses; witness says strong horseshoe magnets are parts of electrical resonance circuits involved in charging.
2. **Hauser 1986/1988 / M6a:** horseshoe magnets with windings, layered perforated/insulating structures, complex grid/magnet/bifilar cylinder assemblies.

4. **Sauder/Snicker relay / unresolved machine:** top allegedly a solenoid with movable tuning contact; Baumann allegedly retuned after partial disassembly.
5. **Cathomen 2001 workshop:** upper path is said to be `strengthened`, associated with increased `capacity`, magnets and likely coils; a Fühler keeps impulses/frequency synchronized; pickups are described as magnetized.

### Round-10 mechanism consequence

The best source-compatible **large/workshop-family research hypothesis** is now:

`electrostatic influence / variable-C frontend`

→ `magnetically biased and/or synchronized pickup network`

→ `tunable inductive/capacitive impedance or resonance conditioning`

→ `nonlinear / possibly reduced-pressure commutation`

→ `Leyden / capacitor storage`.

This is **not** promoted to the M2 historical baseline. It is also not an explanation of the primary energy source. Resonance can accumulate and transform supplied energy; it does not remove the requirement for a real power input.
