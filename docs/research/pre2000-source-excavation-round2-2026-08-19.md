# Pre-2000 Testatika source excavation — round 2

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Scope:** targeted follow-up on the oldest Testatika/Swiss M-L sources, source-authorship conflicts, publication chronology, and missing originals.  
**Rule:** no later mirror is counted as a new witness; event date, publication date and theory author remain separate.

## 1. P. H. Matthey, 20-Oct-1984: stronger report provenance, but edited-container caution

The surviving 1985 Nieper container is stronger than a bare `Nieper heard from Matthey` relay. In the section `The Swiss ML-Converter — A Masterpiece of Craftsmanship and Electronic Engineering`, Nieper writes that Swiss electronics engineer **P. H. Matthey**, DVS member, saw the converter on **20 October 1984** with several Swiss engineers and explicitly introduces a sentence as something Matthey `comments ... in his report`.

The surviving section also identifies Matthey elsewhere as an engineer in **Neuchâtel, Switzerland**.

Later bibliographies independently cite a 1985 item under Matthey's name, commonly as:

`P. H. Matthey, The Swiss ML Converter — A Masterpiece of Craftsmanship and Electronic Engineering, in H. A. Nieper (ed.), Revolution in Technology, Medicine and Society, 1985.`

Some later references even list `L.L., P.H. Matthey` together. This does **not** settle the exact original authorship layout, because the surviving Nieper pages contain unmistakable Nieper first-person/editorial prose around Matthey's quoted comment.

### Updated evidence rule

Use:

- **W1/H1-Matthey-preserved-through-Nieper** for the existence of Matthey's visit/report and the sentence explicitly attributed to his report;
- **NIEPER** for the surrounding tachyon/gravity-stressing interpretation unless the original Matthey report says the same;
- **UNKNOWN** for exact chapter/report authorship until the original German source/report is acquired.

## 2. Pierre H. Matthey identity bridge to a 1989 raum & zeit article

A historical literature index lists:

`Freie Energie ist in uns — nicht in Maschinen`
`Dipl.-Ing. Pierre H. Matthey, Neuchâtel, Schweiz`
`raum & zeit, 37/88, S. 94-96`

The current official `raum & zeit` archive has the same article title in **Ausgabe 37, Jahrgang 1989** and names the author **Pierre M. Matthey** in the preview.

Because the older index gives the full technical title `Dipl.-Ing. Pierre H. Matthey, Neuchâtel`, matching the P. H. Matthey/Neuchâtel identity in Nieper, the identity link is **strong**, but the middle initial and year are not perfectly consistent across catalogues.

### Date conflict

- historical literature index: `37/88`;
- official publisher archive: **issue 37, year 1989**.

Use the official publisher archive as the preferred publication year and preserve `37/88` as a bibliographic conflict, not as a silent correction.

### Why the article matters

The publisher preview says Matthey remains convinced that `free energy` exists but criticizes attempts to obtain it through various little machines and argues for an inward/philosophical first step. Without the full article, this must **not** be called a retraction of his 1984 Testatika report. It does show that his later public position cannot simply be represented by Nieper's 1985 machine enthusiasm.

Sources:
- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/freie-energie-ist-in-uns-nicht-in-maschinen/
- https://www.datadiwan.de/moch/moch_1d.htm

Acquisition target: full issue-37 article, especially any explicit reference to Testatika or his 1984 visit.

## 3. A claimed 1980 Nieper/Testatika date is currently a provenance conflict, not an early source

A modern Testatika page quotes/attributes to Marinov the statement that the first man who admitted to him that he had seen and tested Testatika was Dr. Hans Nieper `at his conference in Hannover`, followed by the page author's parenthetical annotation `im November 1980`.

This is **not sufficient to establish a 1980 Testatika inspection**:

- the `November 1980` wording is visibly an annotator/editor parenthesis on the modern page;
- Nieper's own 1985 first-person account explicitly says **`I myself inspected it on October 28, 1984`**;
- the same book discusses the Hannover 1980 gravity-field-energy conference in other contexts, but the recovered Testatika section itself gives 1983/1984 DVS inspections and Nieper's 1984 date.

### Current ruling

`Nieper/Testatika in November 1980` = **CONFLICT / SECONDARY LEAD**, not accepted chronology.

The earliest currently source-secure event remains the **1983 DVS-inspection relay**, with the earliest detailed visitor records in **1984**.

Sources:
- https://nuetec-forschung.de/Thesta-Distatika/Testatika.html
- https://www.scribd.com/document/131614540/33883875-Nieper-Conversion-of-Gravity-Field-Energy-Revolution-in-Technology-Medicine-and-Society-1985

Acquisition target: the exact Marinov TWT-V page behind the modern quotation and any original 1980 Nieper conference programme/material mentioning Methernitha/Testatika.

## 4. Adolf Schneider, `Energien aus dem Kosmos`, 1989: earlier Testatika publication lead than the 1994 Schneider book

Multiple bibliographies identify:

`Adolf Schneider, Energien aus dem Kosmos — Theoretische und praktische Grundlagen einer neuen Technologie, Jupiter-Verlag, Thun, 1989.`

A later Schneider book specifically cites **page 29** of the 1989 book in its Testatika references. Konstantin Meyl later also cites the same `A. Schneider ... 1989, S. 29` when discussing Testatika.

A bibliographic catalogue describes the 1989 edition as about 74 A4 pages. The work is also listed by the German Digital Library as an Adolf Schneider title.

### Current status

This upgrades `Energien aus dem Kosmos` to a **high-priority pre-1990 Testatika source target**, but not yet to a mechanism source because page 29 itself has not been recovered in this audit.

Evidence class: **A1/BIBLIOGRAPHIC — exact Testatika page pending**.

## 5. The 1989 institutional Methernitha soundtrack remains the highest-value missing operator text

The published bibliography is specific:

`Methernitha (1989), Informationsfilm Thesta-Distatica: Sound Track Transcription`, `Tagungsband Proceedings, Internationaler Kongress für Freie Energie, Einsiedeln, Switzerland 1989`, ISBN `3-9520025-1-8`.

This is unusually valuable because it is an **institutional self-description** rather than a later visitor circuit theory.

Relinfo's May-1990 near-contemporary account independently says Francis Bosshard presented Testatika before about 500 congress attendees and describes his conviction that it was a converter which caught freely available energy and converted it into practical energy.

Marinov's TWT-VII material also places Methernitha representatives and a Testatika presentation at the Einsiedeln congress.

### Priority

Recovering the original proceedings transcription is more valuable than collecting another dozen modern Testatika websites.

## 7. Direct Marinov position in late 1990: still belief, not recovered mechanism

TWT-VII preserves a dated **20 November 1990** text in which Marinov urges another physicist to visit Methernitha/Testatika and continues to call Testatika a perpetuum mobile/free-energy machine.

This matters primarily as a chronology control:

- by late 1990 Marinov remained publicly convinced of the anomalous claim;
- it does **not** override his earlier statement that he had not understood/reproduced the principle;
- Marinov's own source theory remains distinct from operator disclosure.

## 9. Source-order consequences after round 2

The best-supported chronology now looks like:

- **1983:** DVS inspection(s) mentioned by Nieper — original reports missing;
- **17 Mar 1984:** L.L. detailed witness report;
- **20 Oct 1984:** P. H. Matthey visit/report, preserved within Nieper and cited by later bibliographies;
- **28 Oct 1984:** Nieper direct self-reported inspection;
- **1985:** earliest currently recovered substantial printed container of these records;
- **14 Feb 1986:** Hauser visit, first publication May 1986;
- **Aug 1987:** Kelly `Magnets` article, original issue still missing;
- **9 Feb / Jun 1988:** direct Methernitha letter + Marinov visit/lack-of-understanding statement;
- **1989:** Schneider book source lead, Matthey raum & zeit article, Marinov/raum & zeit, institutional SAFE soundtrack;
- **May/Winter 1990:** Relinfo + `Spuren` independent Swiss publication trail;
- **Jan/Feb 1991:** Hartmann direct-author mirror of personal visit and his own air/environment theory;
- **1991:** Kelly/Bailey IECEC synthesis;
- **1992:** Hartmann direct Usenet variable-C/electrostatic comparison;
- **1994:** Schneider detailed retrospective of 1984 visit;
- **1999:** Holzherr direct correspondence and NET-Journal source lead.

The claimed **1980 Nieper/Testatika** date is deliberately excluded from the accepted chronology until the original Marinov/Nieper evidence is recovered.

## 10. What the oldest sources actually add to the mechanism question

After removing duplicated reprints and later theory contamination, one technical convergence survives remarkably early:

`electrostatic influence / charge separation`
`+ magnetic impulse/timing structures`
`+ resonance / conditioning`
`+ capacitor/Leyden storage`.

The strongest early anchor is the 17-Mar-1984 witness report's combination of ~60 rpm, magnetic impulse synchronization, strong horseshoe magnets and electrical-resonance-circuit wording. Hauser's 1986 line independently adds layered perforated/insulating structures and complex magnet/winding assemblies.

What **does not** emerge from the pre-2000 primary/near-primary record is a consistent disclosed bulk reservoir. `tachyon/gravity`, `charged air`, `vacuum/background`, and similar source labels come from different theorists or later interpretations. Methernitha's direct/institutional material located so far says that freely available energy is converted, but the actual real-power coupling mechanism remains withheld/unknown.

That means the energy-source investigation should keep treating:

`environmental reservoir -> missing real-power coupling port -> electrostatic/magnetic/resonant converter -> storage/load`

as a hypothesis to be quantitatively tested, not as an already sourced historical circuit.
