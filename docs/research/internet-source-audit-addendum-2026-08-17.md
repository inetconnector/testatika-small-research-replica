# Internet source audit addendum — primary publications found on 2026-08-17

This addendum records stronger sources found after the initial web-audit ledger was written. It supersedes weaker classifications only where the source chain is demonstrably better. It does not overwrite older notes or collapse different machines.

## A. Kelly & Bailey 1991 IECEC paper — original conference paper located

Direct public PDF:

- Donald Kelly & Patrick G. Bailey, **“The Methernitha Free Energy Machine — The Swiss M-L Converter”**, 26th IECEC, 1991, Vol. 4, pp. 467–472:
  https://www.padrak.com/ine/METHERNITHA_IECEC_1991.pdf

This is substantially more useful than later Kelly compilations because the paper itself exposes its evidence boundary:

- the authors explicitly say their **only real evidence** consisted of documents from researchers who had seen/tested devices plus a videotape;
- they explicitly say all duplication attempts known to them had been unsuccessful;
- the large-disc diameter in their own description is **estimated from photographs**, not measured;
- several component functions are introduced with language such as `thought`, `surmised`, `conjectured` or `appear`;
- they explicitly state that older Searl/Ecklin explanations no longer appeared applicable;
- their flux-enhancement/electron-cascade model is therefore a Kelly/Bailey hypothesis, not recovered Methernitha engineering.

Most importantly, the paper contains a section **“Solicited Opposing Views”**. That section says, in substance:

- lack of technical detail prevents classification;
- the crystal-diode/magnet rectification interpretation is suspicious;
- Poggendorff's effect is self-starting, not proof of self-running;
- incandescent-lamp and heating-wire demonstrations are poor average-power indicators in high-voltage/high-frequency discharge conditions;
- magnetic “flux enhancement” cannot be treated as a power gain without an energy balance.

### Repository consequence

Kelly remains a valuable source-finder/photo-reconstruction author, but **not a first-hand machine witness**. The 1991 paper itself now documents that limitation. Kelly's 20–24-inch disc estimate and six-pickup interpretation must not outrank direct Hauser/Marinov evidence.

The paper's references also provide an exact acquisition target:

- **“More About the Swiss M-L Converter: Pros and Cons,” Magnets Magazine, pp. 19–26, December 1988.**

## B. Hans Nieper 1985 book — actual Testatika pages located

Public book mirror:

- Hans A. Nieper, *Revolution in Technology, Medicine and Society — Conversion of Gravity Field Energy*, English edition/mirror:
  https://www.scribd.com/document/131614540/33883875-Nieper-Conversion-of-Gravity-Field-Energy-Revolution-in-Technology-Medicine-and-Society-1985

The relevant book section states that:

- Swiss engineer P. H. Matthey saw the converter on **20 October 1984** with other Swiss engineers;
- Nieper says he himself inspected it on **28 October 1984**;
- the book then reproduces a separate report titled **“Inspection of the Swiss Tachyon Converter on 17 March 1984”**, signed only `L. L., Rorschach, Switzerland`.

The witness identity must remain exactly that: **L. L.**, unless an independent source resolves the initials.

## C. L. L., Rorschach — 17 March 1984 direct-witness publication

The report published in Nieper's book describes a concrete machine with the following **observed/reported geometry**:

- symmetric apparatus about **110 cm wide × 45 cm deep × 60 cm high**;
- about **20 kg** without the clear acrylic cover;
- acrylic body, light-metal lattice, insulated copper wiring, a component called a crystal-diode rectifier, gold-plated connections;
- **two acrylic discs about 45 cm diameter**, close and counterrotating;
- a fan-like pattern of **50 lattice-electrode/segment positions**;
- about **60 rpm**, described as synchronized by magnetic impulses;
- separately adjustable lattice electrodes for charge extraction;
- a central disk about **10 cm** diameter with rainbow-like appearance;
- finger/hand startup followed by reported continued rotation;
- lamp/heater demonstrations and 300 V / 10 A claims.

The same report also contains **interpretations** that are not established observations: activated air molecules, neutron extraction, gravity/tachyon energy and horseshoe-magnet resonance as the causal explanation. Those interpretations remain witness theory, not CAD facts.

### Machine identity decision

This report is close in dimensions and wording to the later-published Schneider/Weber 13-March-1984 account, but the dates differ by four days and the publication chains may share text or describe the same machine. Independence is not proven.

It is therefore assigned provisionally to **M5a** rather than being counted as an independent confirmation of M5.

## D. Schneider / Hans Weber — 13 March 1984 line strengthened

A September/October 2011 NET-Journal article reproduces material attributed to Inge Schneider's 1994 brochure *Neue Technologien zur Freien Energie*. Public e-paper mirror:

- https://www.yumpu.com/de/document/view/4114787/paul-baumann-erbauer-der-energiemaschine-testatika-93jahrig-

The quoted account identifies the visit as **13 March 1984**, with Dr. sc. nat. Hans Weber and Inge Schneider (then Schönthal). It independently preserves essentially the same physical envelope as the L. L. report:

- slightly over 1 m wide, 45 cm deep, 60 cm high, ~20 kg without acrylic cover;
- acrylic, light-metal grids, insulated copper conductors, crystal-diode rectifier terminology, gold-plated terminals;
- non-contact pickup;
- counterrotating discs hand-started;
- central ~10-cm rainbow-coloured disc;
- lamp/heater demonstration and an operator-stated 300 V / 10 A figure.

The account says Hans Weber was allowed to lift the machine and look underneath and saw no battery/external supply. This is a **limited concealment check**, not a closed energy balance and not proof that no stored/hidden source existed elsewhere.

Because the L. L. report and Schneider account are extraordinarily similar, this audit does **not** double-count them as independent confirmation until their text/source relationship is established.

## E. 2010 Methernitha reply published by the recipient

The same 2011 NET-Journal article states that the editors asked Methernitha in 2010 about further Testatika development and prints the response. In substance, Methernitha answered that:

- the alternative-energy research group no longer existed;
- the Thestatika could no longer be shown;
- Internet entries about it had been made without their knowledge;
- they believed the machine had motivated others to pursue alternatives.

This is stronger provenance than a forum quotation because it is presented by the correspondence recipient in a dated publication. It is still a **published correspondence reproduction**, not a currently accessible official Methernitha webpage.

### Consequence

An Internet page using Methernitha photographs or prose must not be assumed to be officially authorized merely from appearance.

## F. 1999 engineer-demonstration date is unresolved, not normalized

Three public source chains conflict:

1. a 2004 Testatika seminar program says **34 engineers / former Burgdorf Technikum students, 5 June 1999**;
2. the later Schneider NET-Journal article says **30 Swiss engineers, 4 August 1999**;
3. Holzherr/Hartmann web copies carry early-August 1999 translation/email dates, which may describe publication/translation rather than the visit date.

These may refer to different events, or one later date may have been conflated with publication. Until an original invitation, attendee list, dated photo/video or contemporaneous report fixes it, the event date is **CONFLICT**.

Do not rewrite all 1999 sources to one date.

## G. Principle Experiment — additional low-distance geometry lead

A Rimstar preservation page reports additional correspondence details for the Principle Experiment:

- the base beneath the swinging arm was described as a **flattened hemisphere**, like half an electric bell;
- the two connected capacitors were approximately **8 cm high and 3–4 cm in diameter**;
- Baumann reportedly said some capacitors were purchased and some made on site, and that this distinction was not important.

Source:

- https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/principle_experiment.htm

This is useful for M0 reconstruction, but the web page is still a correspondence/preservation relay. It is a **geometry lead**, not yet the same evidentiary level as a dated original scan.

An August 1987 Kelly/Magnets Testatika item is repeatedly referenced but its exact original page range is still to be acquired.

## I. Current strongest conclusion after this addendum

The crawl has materially improved **provenance, machine taxonomy, geometry constraints and experimental controls**, but it still has not produced:

- an authentic complete M2 or M6 node-to-node circuit;
- a verified original crystal material/I-V curve;
- the exact M2 wire-through-disc route;
- the M6a cylinder node topology;
- a closed historical net-energy measurement.

Those remain explicit unknowns. The richer Internet record increases the number of documented claims and variants; it does not justify turning any one speculative theory into the original mechanism.
