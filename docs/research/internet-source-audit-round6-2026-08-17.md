# Internet source audit — round 6 — 2026-08-17

## Scope

Round 6 continues the provenance-first crawl with emphasis on source revision history and recoverable early digital archives. The main new result is not a new hidden circuit: it is a direct correction by Don Kelly that invalidates one influential early reconstruction detail, plus several archive/film acquisition paths that can still yield higher-quality historical material.

No CAD geometry is changed from this round alone. A secondary author's corrected drawing is evidence about that author's reconstruction history, not an automatic statement about a particular Methernitha machine.

## 1. Don Kelly explicitly retracted an influential early horseshoe-magnet/disc depiction

Primary-author letter as published in *New Energy News*:

- Don Kelly, “Dis-Information in the New Energy Field: Specifically on the Swiss M-L Converter (Testatika),” NEN Vol. 6 No. 4, Aug. 1998, pp. 17–18:
  https://www.padrak.com/ine/NEN_6_4_12.html

Kelly says an Internet depiction of the twin electrostatic discs with **two horseshoe magnets and windings in close interacting proximity to the discs** came from an early `Magnets` study and that these early details were **later shown to be incorrect**.

### Consequence

Any Testatika reconstruction which imports that specific Kelly horseshoe-magnet/disc arrangement must now be labelled:

`KELLY-EARLY-RECONSTRUCTION / AUTHOR-LATER-CORRECTED`.

It must not be used to override direct Hauser, Marinov, Holzherr or machine-specific video evidence.

This does **not** mean horseshoe magnets were absent from all Testatika variants. Direct small-machine evidence independently supports horseshoe magnets for M2. The correction is specifically about the Kelly depiction/placement near the twin discs.

## 2. Kelly's `Magnets` date is itself a provenance conflict

Kelly's 1998 letter describes the corrected source as an early study in `Magnets` magazine of approximately **1984**.

By contrast, the official `raum&zeit` page for Kelly's later German article states that Kelly described the Testatika in `Magnets` in **August 1987** and that the electro-schematic diagram was reproduced in `raum&zeit`.

Official publisher page:

- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/der-schweizer-ml-konverter/

Possible explanations include:

1. Kelly remembered the year imprecisely in 1998;
2. there were two different `Magnets` articles/versions;
3. the later German reproduction came from a revised 1987 version rather than the earlier study he was recalling.

Until the original magazine pages are acquired, the repository records:

`KELLY MAGNETS SOURCE DATE/VERSION = CONFLICT (approx. 1984 self-recollection vs Aug-1987 publisher attribution)`.

Do not normalize these dates.

## 3. Kelly's radioactive-material rejection is corroborative history, not independent Methernitha proof

In the same 1998 letter Kelly rejects a then-circulating Internet claim that radium chloride was used, saying the information he had received over roughly fifteen years never indicated radioactive material was used or contemplated.

This is consistent with the stronger Baumann→Holzherr report already in the repository that radium chloride was not the energy source.

Evidence handling:

- Kelly statement = `KELLY SOURCE-HISTORY / SECONDARY CORROBORATION`;
- Baumann→Holzherr remains the more direct operator-source chain;
- no radioactive material is introduced into the replica or experiments.

## 11. Search result versus source independence rule reinforced

This round repeatedly found many copies of the same Hauser and Testatika texts in Hungarian, English, Korean, forum and aggregator mirrors.

A higher mirror count does **not** increase evidentiary confidence when the text lineage is shared.

In particular:

- Hauser translated copies are one transmission family unless independently sourced;
- Hartmann run-instruction copies are one distribution family;
- Kelly-derived schematics copied into later books/sites remain Kelly lineage;
- `Magnets`, `raum&zeit`, Kelly manuals and 1998 Kelly retrospective must be versioned, not merged into a timeless “Kelly says” source.
