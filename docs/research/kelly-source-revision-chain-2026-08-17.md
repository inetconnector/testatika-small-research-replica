# Don Kelly Testatika source-revision chain — 2026-08-17

## Why this file exists

Don Kelly material has been widely recopied into Testatika diagrams. The public record now shows that Kelly's own interpretation changed over time. Therefore `Kelly diagram` is not a single stable evidence object.

## Known chronology

### Possible early `Magnets` study — circa 1984 according to Kelly in 1998

In his Aug. 1998 *New Energy News* letter Kelly says a depiction showing twin horseshoe magnets/windings close to the twin electrostatic discs came from an early `Magnets` study around 1984. He explicitly says this early interpretation was later shown to be incorrect.

Source:

- https://www.padrak.com/ine/NEN_6_4_12.html

Status: `AUTHOR-SELF-CORRECTION`. The original circa-1984 `Magnets` pages have not yet been recovered.

### `Magnets`, August 1987 — publisher-confirmed Kelly article/diagram

The official *raum&zeit* catalogue says Kelly described Testatika in `Magnets` in Aug. 1987 and made an electro-schematic diagram that *raum&zeit* reproduced in original form in issue 40/1989.

Source:

- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/der-schweizer-ml-konverter/

Status: `BIBLIOGRAPHICALLY CONFIRMED`, original `Magnets` pages still unacquired.

### `Magnets`, December 1988, pp. 19–26

Kelly & Bailey's 1991 IECEC paper cites:

- `More About the Swiss M-L Converter: Pros and Cons`, *Magnets Magazine*, Dec. 1988, pp. 19–26.

Status: `EXACT REFERENCE`, original pages still unacquired.

### *raum&zeit* 40/1989

The German publisher's archive identifies its Testatika package as containing Marinov material followed by Kelly reports and says the Kelly electro-schematic was reproduced from `Magnets`.

Status: publisher archive is authoritative for its own publication history, not for Testatika physical correctness.

### Kelly & Bailey, 26th IECEC, 1991, pp. 467–472

The paper explicitly states that the authors' real evidence came from material supplied by researchers who had seen/tested machines plus a videotape; it also says known duplication attempts had failed. Photo-derived dimensions and conjectured component functions therefore remain reconstruction, not direct observation.

Public PDF locator already recorded in the audit:

- https://www.padrak.com/ine/METHERNITHA_IECEC_1991.pdf

### Kelly derivative design, 1992

Kelly's `An Enhanced Quad-Disc Electrostatic Generator`, 27th IECEC/SAE paper 929472, is a proposed derivative design inspired by the Swiss converter. It is not a recovered historical Testatika schematic.

- DOI: 10.4271/929472

Status: `KELLY-DERIVED-HYPOTHESIS`.

### Kelly 1998 self-correction and prototype-build period

Aug. 1998: Kelly explicitly rejects the early horseshoe-near-disc depiction as incorrect.

Nov. 1998: *New Energy News* reports Kelly/Space Energy Association was building a Swiss M-L-converter prototype. No validated outcome was found in the current crawl.

## Chronology conflict

The 1998 self-correction says the erroneous `Magnets` study was around 1984, while *raum&zeit* identifies a Kelly `Magnets` article in Aug. 1987. Until original issues are acquired, do **not** assume these are the same article or diagram.

## CAD/evidence rule

1. Kelly geometry never outranks named direct-witness evidence from Marinov, Hauser, Holzherr or a clearly identified contemporaneous observer.
2. Horseshoe magnets independently visible/source-stated on M2 or M6 variants remain valid evidence for those machines; Kelly's self-correction applies only to the Kelly-derived placement it discusses.
3. Any historical CAD change based solely on a Kelly diagram must record the exact publication/date/figure and whether that figure was later superseded.
4. Proposed Kelly 1992+ designs belong in comparison/experiment space, not in the historical baseline.
