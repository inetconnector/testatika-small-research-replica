# Hidden-input witness constraints — 2026-08-18

## Purpose

V4.11 quantitatively excludes several weak ambient reservoirs as direct ~100-W tabletop sources. The next conventional question is whether historical demonstrations could have received power through an overlooked **local** path: base/table, hidden wire, battery/storage, mechanical coupling or strong near-field transmission.

This note records witness statements that constrain those possibilities. They do **not** prove anomalous energy. Visual inspection, lifting and moving a machine are weaker than a controlled teardown plus simultaneous energy balance.

---

## 1. M5 / Weber-Schneider 13-Mar-1984: machine reportedly lifted and underside inspected

Later public reproductions of Inge Schneider's first-person 1984 account state that the physicist accompanying her was allowed to **lift the machine and look underneath**, and that no battery or externally connected power source was seen. A Nuetec reproduction separately attributes to Hans Weber a statement that he was allowed to lift the machine, inspect underneath, noticed its substantial weight, and found no batteries or external power sources.

Public preservation routes:

- https://www.yumpu.com/de/document/view/4114787/paul-baumann-erbauer-der-energiemaschine-testatika-93jahrig-
- https://nuetec-forschung.de/Thesta-Distatika/Testatika.html

### Evidence classification

- original 13-Mar-1984 visit: **W1/P1 historical witness event**;
- wording available here: **later republication / quotation of the witness account**, not the original 1984 notebook;
- `no battery / no external connection visible`: **inspection statement**, not a closed energy audit.

### What this constrains

For the M5 witness machine, a **gross obvious cable from the underside/table** or an openly visible battery is less compatible with the published witness account.

### What it does not constrain

The observation does not exclude:

- internal capacitive/electret/chemical storage;
- an internal concealed battery not recognized during a non-destructive lift;
- non-contact capacitive/inductive/RF coupling;
- coupling through output/instrument wires or a support not inspected electrically;
- stored energy sufficient for a short demonstration;
- incorrect interpretation of lamp/heater brightness or electrical ratings;
- an unmeasured mechanical or environmental input.

No calibrated duration/input/output/storage ledger accompanies the lifting statement.

## 2. Holzherr 1999: small ~12-cm models could reportedly be lifted while running

Hans Holzherr's 1999 interview/report says the protected ~50-cm demonstration machine could not be touched or lifted, so he could not determine whether its apparently solid base contained anything. He then distinguishes the smaller ~12-cm models, saying visitors **could lift and examine them while their discs kept turning**.

Public preservation:

- https://www.novakcorp.com/energy/experiments/tesnews.htm

### Evidence classification

**P1 direct visitor report**, but specifically for Holzherr's M4/~12-cm small-model family, not Marinov M2 unless a source bridge proves identity.

### Consequence

A fixed galvanic table feed is less compatible with the observed running state of those liftable M4 models. This is a valuable cross-machine constraint, but it cannot be imported into M2 or the 50-cm M6b machine.

It also does not exclude finite stored charge, internal storage, non-contact field coupling or an unrecognized internal source.

## 3. Hauser retrospective: machine reportedly moved to test hidden-transmitter/focus hypothesis

Albert Hauser later wrote that his group investigated the machine for hours and **changed its physical location** to test whether a hidden transmitter or receiving focus could explain the demonstration.

Public author-page preservation:

- https://equapio.com/energie/testatika-legendaere-energiemaschine-der-methernitha/

The same retrospective is the source for Baumann-attributed atmosphere/ion/window/storm statements. It contains corrupted numeric transcriptions elsewhere, so it is used for qualitative witness history only.

### What this constrains

If the location-change report is accurate and the machine continued to perform similarly, it weakens a hypothesis requiring one precise fixed geometric focus in the original room.

### What it does not constrain

It does not exclude:

- a broad-area RF field;
- a transmitter near enough that both tested positions remain in its near/far field;
- a source/coupler moving with the apparatus;
- building wiring/ground/common-mode coupling present at both positions;
- internal storage or mechanical input.

No RF spectrum, E/H-field map or input-power measurement from the location-change test is preserved.

## 4. Combined constraint on the 'hidden ordinary source' hypothesis

The three source lines are useful because they attack different gross hidden-input possibilities:

- M5: lift + underside inspection reportedly found no obvious battery/external connection;
- M4: small models reportedly remained running while physically lifted;
- large-family Hauser retrospective: location reportedly changed to test a fixed hidden-transmitter focus.

However, they concern **different machines/dates** and must not be combined as though one single machine passed all three tests.

The defensible cross-machine conclusion is narrower:

> Some historical observers did make nontrivial attempts to exclude obvious external feeds. Those attempts reduce the plausibility of a simple visible wire or fixed under-table battery explanation for the specific witnessed variants, but they do not constitute a closed test for non-contact coupling, internal storage, concealed sources or measurement error.

## 5. Experimental consequence for the replica programme

A modern test can close what the historical inspections could not.

For any apparent autonomous run, the fixture should be tested in four boundary states while preserving one-variable-at-a-time discipline:

1. mechanically isolated from the table except a characterized dielectric support;
2. electrically isolated with all instrumentation fiber/isolated and all parasitic earth capacitances estimated/measured;
3. operated inside a conductive enclosure with a separately controlled internal reference electrode, followed by a nonconductive geometric dummy enclosure;
4. moved/rotated between positions while logging wideband E/H fields, base displacement current, shaft power, storage energy and load power continuously.

The purpose is not to reproduce a historical claim by assumption. It is to determine whether a local conventional input port follows the machine, follows the room, follows the table, or disappears under controlled boundary changes.

## 6. Current implication

After V4.11 and these witness constraints, the most interesting unresolved conventional candidate is no longer **ordinary ambient** atmosphere/RF/geomagnetism. It is a more local and structured input path:

`building/table/base/chassis common-mode or near-field coupling`

possibly acted on by

`electrostatic influence + magnetized/timed pickup + tuned impedance + nonlinear commutation`.

That remains a **HYPOTHESIS** until real incoming power is measured. Resonance can make a weak signal conspicuous, but it cannot turn micro/nanowatt ambient input into sustained 100-W real output without another source term.
