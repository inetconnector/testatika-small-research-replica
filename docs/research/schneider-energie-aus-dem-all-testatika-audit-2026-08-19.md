# Schneider 2000 Testatika source audit — 2026-08-19

## Scope

Source reviewed: Adolf and Inge Schneider, *Energie aus dem All — Das Geheimnis einer neuen Energiequelle* (publication framing dated August 2000 in the book).

Local source artifact supplied for audit:

- filename: `61388011-Schneider-Adolf-Inge-Energie-Aus-Dem-All.pdf`
- pages: 362 PDF pages
- size: 6,566,077 bytes
- SHA-256: `f4388b3307a5bf7033e4bc4ed441fd8a6b4cf41c96f7c2d9fbe1d6cb627dec45`

This audit records only Testatika-relevant material and keeps four levels separate:

1. first-person/eyewitness statements by the Schneiders;
2. claims attributed by them to an inventor or another researcher;
3. later reconstruction material reproduced in the book;
4. project interpretation.

The PDF itself is not added to the repository; only bibliographic/provenance notes and derived source records are stored.

## 1. Summer-1984 Schneider visit: important retrospective eyewitness source

Inge Schneider writes that in **summer 1984** she entered a residential/work community in the canton of Bern together with an unnamed **atomic physicist**. They were received by an inventor identified only as **`P.B.`**. The book later explicitly identifies the demonstrated machine as the **Testatika of Methernitha**.

Source classification:

- event date: summer 1984;
- publication date: 2000;
- class: **W1-retrospective** for what Schneider says she personally saw;
- caveat: approximately sixteen years elapsed between event and this publication;
- `P.B.` must not be expanded to a full identity unless a closer source does so;
- the accompanying atomic physicist is unnamed in this source.

This is therefore a strong source for the existence of a Schneider-described 1984 demonstration, but not a contemporaneous 1984 laboratory report.

## 2. Machine geometry and visible components described by Schneider

The 1984 machine is described as approximately:

- a little over **1 m wide**;
- about **0.5 m high**;
- two **rear-to-front / one-behind-the-other acrylic-glass discs**;
- **silver-coated lamellae/segments** on the discs;
- an acrylic cover;
- **light-metal grids**;
- insulated copper conductors;
- a **crystal-diode rectifier** (`Kristalldioden-Gleichrichter`);
- gold-plated connection poles.

A centrally mounted disc of about **10 cm diameter** is also described as shimmering in rainbow colours while the machine was running.

Project effect:

- the dual-disc + segmented conductor + grid + rectifier cluster is independently relevant to the electrostatic/charge-transfer architecture already under study;
- the explicit `crystal-diode rectifier` is a concrete historical use of crystal terminology for a semiconductor/rectifier function.

Guardrail: this does **not** justify equating every later `crystal` statement with an ordinary diode. It is one source-secure crystal/rectifier instance only.

## 3. Start sequence: source explicitly describes a charge-up phase

Schneider says the inventor set the two discs into **counter-rotation by a light fingertip motion** and continued until the converter had **charged sufficiently** (`so weit aufgeladen`), after which it continued to run quietly and without an external power connection visible to the observers.

This wording is important because it is not merely a statement that a flywheel was spun up. The source itself frames the transition as an **electrical charge-up state**.

Project interpretation — **HYPOTHESIS, not source-stated mechanism**:

`manual counter-rotation -> electrostatic charge state builds -> threshold/working state reached -> continued cyclic operation`

This is compatible with the project's existing `charge-state regeneration` / cyclic electrostatic commutation line, but it does not prove that model or identify the bulk energy source.

## 4. Output claim: 300 V DC / 10 A

Immediately after the start description, the book states that after a few seconds **300 V DC at 10 A** could be taken from the output poles.

If both values were simultaneous RMS/DC load values, the nominal electrical power would be:

`P = V I = 300 V * 10 A = 3000 W`.

The same passage says a lamp or heating rod was connected, that the lamp was very bright, and the heating rod became too hot to touch after a short time.

Evidence boundary:

- retain **300 V / 10 A** as a historical source claim;
- do **not** upgrade it to a verified 3-kW measurement;
- the book gives no closed energy balance, calibrated instrument list, waveform record, four-wire/load metrology, source-impedance test or uncertainty budget here;
- the statement that the source could run for hours/months/years is explicitly framed as what the inventor said, not as a duration measurement performed by Schneider in this visit.

## 5. Physical inspection: useful but limited anti-concealment evidence

The accompanying physicist is said to have been allowed to lift the machine and look underneath, checking that no battery or external power source was visible.

This is useful historical evidence against an **obvious** external cable or loose battery in the inspected area.

It is not a closed-source exclusion test. It does not by itself rule out:

- internal finite storage;
- concealed conductors elsewhere in the structure;
- capacitive/inductive/environmental coupling;
- previously charged storage;
- measurement error or incomplete load metrology.

Repository wording should therefore remain: **limited visual inspection, not a closed power-balance proof**.

## 6. 10-cm iridescent central disc: geometry lead

The approximately 10-cm central rainbow-shimmering disc is a useful image-matching lead.

No mechanism is assigned to it in this audit. Conventional optical possibilities such as reflective/diffractive/interference effects remain open. It should be cross-checked against dated photographs and film frames before any electrical function is inferred.

## 7. Nelson Camus / radium line: keep separate from Swiss Testatika

In the later Nelson Camus section, the book reports Camus's story of a machine in a Mennonite community in Chile that he later associated with the name Testatika. The text says his investigation of that Chilean machine led him to a **radium** explanation and that he then projected that explanation onto the Swiss Testatika.

Crucially, the Schneiders themselves state that he was **probably not correct** in doing so.

Therefore:

- Chilean machine claim: separate source chain;
- Camus radium interpretation of Swiss Testatika: **HYPOTHESIS / low confidence**;
- no radium source term is added to the canonical Methernitha mechanism;
- no radioactive replication experiment is justified by this material.

## 8. Camus reconstruction drawing reproduced on printed p.188

The PDF page corresponding to printed book page **188** reproduces a drawing captioned in German:

`Radiumgedopfte Testatika-Version nach N. Camus`

The drawing itself is headed:

`SWISS ML MACHINE - ELECTRICAL SCHEMATIC`

Visible labels include:

- `TWIN DISK`
- `STANDARD WIMSHURST CAPACITOR`
- `STEEL SEGMENTS FOR 60HZ DC OUTPUT AT 60 RPM`
- `COLLECTOR BRUSHES`
- `POGGENDORF MOTOR BRUSHES`
- `HORSESHOE PERMANENT MAGNET WITH COIL`
- `DIODE CAPACITOR`
- `HV input`
- `low voltage output`
- two `LEYDEN JAR` blocks
- `SPARK GAP`
- `RADIUM DOPED INDUCTOR-CAPACITOR THAT MAKES NUCLEAR BATTERY`

This is highly useful as a **reconstruction-source map**, because it places dual discs, segmented timing, brushes, horseshoe magnets/coils, diode/capacitor, HV, Leyden storage and spark-gap switching in one historical reconstruction.

But source classification must remain:

- **M3 / reconstruction / Camus-associated hypothesis**;
- not an original Methernitha schematic;
- not evidence that the Swiss machine used radium;
- not evidence that every labelled component existed in the demonstrated 1984 machine.

The `60 Hz ... at 60 RPM` label is especially useful as a historical hypothesis for approximately 60 electrical segment events per one-second revolution, but it remains reconstruction evidence rather than a measured operating specification of the Methernitha machine.

## 9. Provenance lead for the Camus drawing

The book's image credits identify printed page **188** as sourced from the brochure:

`Methernitha Testatica`, Energy Research Co.

This brochure is a high-priority acquisition target. It may clarify:

- who drew the schematic;
- whether Camus supplied it directly;
- which labels came from observation versus reconstruction;
- the origin of the 60-rpm / 60-Hz statement;
- the origin of `Poggendorff motor brushes`, horseshoe magnet/coil, Leyden-jar and spark-gap labels.

Do not treat the drawing as a Methernitha-origin schematic until that provenance is resolved.

## 11. Hartmann provenance note from the same book

The book's web-resource section describes `overunity.de` as an **Overunity database by Stefan Hartmann**, and elsewhere names the Overunity homepage of Dipl.-Ing. Stefan Hartmann.

This is useful as a contemporaneous c.2000 source for Hartmann's role as an Overunity documentation/web node.

Guardrail remains unchanged:

- this does not show Hartmann filmed the known Testatika film;
- film provenance remains separate from his site/operator role and from his personal-visit source chain.

## 12. Effect on current mechanism model

The 2000 book does **not** solve the bulk-energy question.

What it strengthens historically is the architecture cluster:

`manual start / counter-rotation`

`-> electrostatic charge-up state`

`-> segmented dual discs + grids`

`-> rectification / switching`

`-> capacitor/Leyden storage`

`-> load and possible electromechanical feedback`

The Camus drawing adds an M3 reconstruction cluster:

`segment timing + horseshoe magnet/coil + diode/capacitor + HV + Leyden jars + spark gap`.

These clusters may be compared with the Cathomen `Klapf`, sensor/synchronisation and cyclic charge-state-regeneration evidence, but they must not be merged into a single source-stated schematic.

The primary real-power reservoir remains **UNKNOWN**.

## Repository guardrails added by this audit

1. Do not promote 300 V / 10 A to verified 3-kW output without metrology.
2. Do not promote the claimed long-duration operation to Schneider-observed duration.
3. Do not identify `P.B.` beyond the source text without a closer source.
4. Do not equate all `crystal` references with semiconductor diodes.
5. Do not import Camus's radium hypothesis into the canonical Swiss Testatika model.
6. Do not treat the Camus/Energy Research schematic as a Methernitha original.
7. Preserve the 1984 event / 2000 publication-date distinction.
8. Keep the energy-source field **UNKNOWN** until a closed-balance source channel is identified.
