# Internet source audit — round 7 — 2026-08-17

## Scope

Round 7 concentrates on operator-adjacent language, chronology of 1989 film presentations, and two later book/corpus lines that may preserve unpublished historical material. It does **not** identify a new authentic circuit and therefore does not change CAD.

## 1. Francis Bosshard 1994 interview: substantial recipient-side reproduction located

A September/October 2011 NET-Journal article reproduces a substantial interview that the editors say they conducted with **Francis Bosshard in 1994**. The article identifies Bosshard as responsible for Methernitha public relations.

Public e-paper mirror:

- https://www.yumpu.com/de/document/view/4114787/paul-baumann-erbauer-der-energiemaschine-testatika-93jahrig-

The reproduced interview contains two distinct evidence layers:

### 1.1 Bosshard's own 1994 explanations

Bosshard explains why Methernitha would not release the Testatika and frames this primarily as a religious/social decision. These statements are useful for historical motivation/provenance, not for engineering.

### 1.2 Baumann language relayed by Bosshard

Bosshard says Baumann once told him, in substance, that they never did anything that nature did not want. He then gives a more specific electron-language formulation: they did not make electrons do what they did not want; rather they tried to find what the electrons wanted and made those conditions easier.

Evidence chain:

`Paul Baumann → Francis Bosshard → Schneider/NET-Journal interview 1994 → publication/reproduction 2011`.

Classification:

- `H1/H2 OPERATOR-ADJACENT LANGUAGE`;
- valuable for reconstructing Baumann's explanatory vocabulary;
- **not** a literal microscopic model of electron behaviour;
- **not** a wiring diagram;
- **not** independent proof of an anomalous energy source.

This language should remain separate from:

- Marinov's direct `ANOTHER language` statement;
- Holzherr's report that Baumann used non-scientific terminology;
- Methernitha's own film vocabulary (`Taster`, cloud/earth analogy, etc.).

The sources converge only on the fact that Baumann/Methernitha often used non-standard explanatory language; they do not yield one uniquely decoded mechanism.

## 2. Two separate 1989 Testatika film-presentation events are now distinguishable

Later Testatika histories often blur different 1989 congress appearances into one event. The crawl now supports at least **two separate film-presentation contexts**.

### 2.1 Jupiter-Verlag congress at Aeschi — April 1989

A current Jupiter-Verlag archival product description independently dates its congress **`Welt und Menschheit im Wandel`** to **April 1989**.

- https://www.jupiter-verlag.ch/shop/detail_neu.php?artikel=25

A 2023 Schneider book/promo text says Francis Bosshard showed a film about the Testatika at a Jupiter-Verlag congress with exactly that title in Aeschi.

- https://www.pravda-tv.com/2023/05/testatika-und-weitere-freie-energie-geraete-video/

Evidence classification:

- congress title + April-1989 date = `A1/PUBLISHER-ARCHIVE`;
- Bosshard/Testatika-film presence = `LATE SCHNEIDER RETROSPECTIVE`, unless the 1989 programme is recovered.

### 2.2 SAFE congress at Einsiedeln — 27–29 October 1989

Already established in Round 3:

- Marinov TWT VII reports a 30-minute Testatika/Methernitha film and Methernitha representatives;
- contemporary Relinfo May 1990 says Testatika was shown there only by film, not as a live machine.

### Corrected chronology

There is no need to force these into one contradictory event. The current chronology is:

- **April 1989, Aeschi/Jupiter:** later Schneider source says Bosshard showed Testatika film;
- **27–29 Oct 1989, Einsiedeln/SAFE:** stronger contemporaneous/direct-author evidence supports a film presentation.

The exact Aeschi programme/session remains an acquisition target.

## 3. 2023 Schneider book is a high-value mixed-provenance acquisition target

Official/current publisher page:

- Adolf & Inge Schneider, `Testatika und weitere Freie-Energie-Geräte`, Jupiter-Verlag, 2023:
  https://www.jupiter-verlag.ch/shop/auswahl_neu.php

The book is especially relevant because **Inge Schneider is part of the direct 13-March-1984 witness line** and the publisher/promo states that the volume contains previously unpublished Testatika material and a chapter drawing on the 1994 Bosshard interview.

However, the book also includes later theories and claims about other alleged free-energy devices. Therefore its evidentiary role must be assigned **page by page**:

- direct first-person 1984 recollection by Inge Schneider → `W1/P1-RETROSPECTIVE`;
- reproduced dated historical documents → rank according to the underlying document;
- Bosshard interview material → `H1/H2 OPERATOR-ADJACENT`;
- later Schneider synthesis → `S2`;
- Wiedergut/Chmela mechanism proposals → `HYPOTHESIS`, not Methernitha engineering;
- ratings such as 300 W–7 kW or an unfinished 30-kW machine → late compilation/operator claims unless a primary source is supplied.

No late book claim is transferred into M2/M6 CAD without an older machine-specific source bridge.

## 6. Pierre M. Matthey: later published position complicates Nieper's portrayal

Nieper's 1985 book says Swiss engineer **P. H. Matthey** inspected the converter on 20 October 1984 and reports Matthey as accepting that the absent primary propulsion could not be conventionally explained. That account remains a later book transmission until Matthey's original report is found.

A later article by Pierre M. Matthey is indexed by the official `raum&zeit` archive:

- `Freie Energie ist in uns – nicht in Maschinen`, issue 37, publisher archive year 1989:
  https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/freie-energie-ist-in-uns-nicht-in-maschinen/

The publisher summary says Matthey remained convinced of free energy in a broad sense but argued against focusing first on machine-building and advocated an inward/philosophical approach.

### Consequence

This does **not** disprove that Matthey visited Testatika in 1984. It does show that:

- Nieper's 1985 presentation of Matthey must not be treated as Matthey's timeless endorsement of a machine-based theory;
- the original **20-Oct-1984 Matthey report** is essential to distinguish what Matthey personally observed from Nieper's tachyon/gravity interpretation;
- later philosophical writing and earlier witness material must be source-separated.

## 7. Bosshard's language must not be converted into a technical electron model

The phrase about making conditions that electrons “want” is precisely the sort of non-standard explanatory language that can invite over-interpretation.

Allowed repository use:

- evidence that Baumann/Bosshard framed the development as finding permissive natural conditions rather than forcing a process;
- motivation for reversible experiments involving field boundary conditions, charge-state relaxation, humidity/ion environment, and low-loss electrostatic operation.

Not allowed:

- treating `electrons want X` as a literal physical law;
- inferring a specific material, topology, polarity, current path or energy source;
- merging it with Hartmann air-ion, Kelly cascade, Frolov vacuum, or other later mechanisms.
