# Pre-2000 Testatika source excavation — round 4

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Focus:** early digital/BBS preservation, exact 1988/1989 bibliography, and separation of institutional technical wording from later reconstructions.

## 1. KeelyNet preserved a distinct early Testatika digital corpus

A surviving KeelyNet/Vangard Sciences energy archive lists `STATIKA1.txt` as the index/container for a set of Testatika files and thanks **Mark Williams** for supplying them. The archive description says the complete Testatika text set was distributed as `STATIKA1.ZIP` in the Energy section.

A separate file, `SWISSML.txt`, is described as a **direct German-to-English translation of a Testatika tape generated by the Methernitha group**, shared to KeelyNet through Brian Prothro.

Sources:
- https://www.newphysics.se/archives/keelynet/energy/
- historical mail-archive snapshot of the KeelyNet index dated 13-Dec-1999: https://www.mail-archive.com/ctrl%40listserv.aol.com/msg31218.html

### Evidence significance

This establishes an independent **pre-2000 digital distribution lineage**:

`Methernitha institutional tape/text -> German/English translation -> KeelyNet/BBS archive -> later web mirrors`.

It does **not** establish that KeelyNet translators independently verified the machine or that the introductory KeelyNet power claims were Methernitha measurements.

The actual `STATIKA1.txt`/`SWISSML.txt` raw bodies were not retrievable through the current web cache in this audit, so the index metadata is preserved as an acquisition lead rather than silently reconstructed from later copies.

## 2. Do not merge KeelyNet institutional-translation lineage with Hartmann

The KeelyNet index names **Brian Prothro** as the person through whose efforts `SWISSML` was shared and describes the source as a Methernitha-group tape. It does not assign camera authorship to Stefan Hartmann.

Repository rule remains:
- no Testatika film/video is attributed to Hartmann;
- Hartmann's own visit/self-report and his later theory/distribution roles are separate source lines.

## 3. Exact December-1988 `Magnets Magazine` source is now hard-bibliographic

The recovered 1991 Kelly/Bailey IECEC paper gives the missing citation precisely:

`More About the Swiss M-L Converter: Pros and Cons`, **Magnets Magazine**, December 1988, pp. 19–26.

This is now an exact acquisition target, not merely an internet-title lead.

The original pages have still not been recovered. Until they are, any later Kelly schematic/functions must be traced to the 1991 paper or other known source, not retroactively assigned to December 1988.

Source:
- https://www.padrak.com/ine/METHERNITHA_IECEC_1991.pdf

## 4. Matthey bibliography conflict is resolved only partially

The official `raum & zeit` archive places `Freie Energie ist in uns – nicht in Maschinen` in **issue 37, year 1989**, and names Pierre M. Matthey in the preview. A historical literature index lists the same title as `Dipl.-Ing. Pierre H. Matthey, Neuchatel`, `raum & zeit 37/88`, pp.94–96.

Preferred publication year: **1989** because it comes from the current publisher archive.  
Preserved conflict: the older index has `37/88` and middle initial `H`.

The preview shows that Matthey still professed belief in `free energy` while criticizing attempts to obtain it through little machines. Without the full article, it is **not** evidence that he retracted his 1984 Testatika observation/report.

Sources:
- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/freie-energie-ist-in-uns-nicht-in-maschinen/
- https://www.datadiwan.de/moch/moch_1d.htm

## 5. `Spuren` no.14 / Winter 1990 is an independent Swiss source trail

Relinfo's May-1990 article says the Zürich periodical `Spuren von Entfaltung, Wandel und Einklang`, no.14, Winter 1990, devoted several pages to Methernitha and Testatika.

This is useful because it is a **Swiss near-contemporary publication line** separate from Nieper/Kelly/US BBS circulation.

Original issue still missing.

Source:
- https://www.relinfo.ch/methernitha/testatikatxt.html

## 6. Schneider 1989 page 29 is repeatedly cross-cited but still not recovered

The existence of Adolf Schneider's 1989 book `Energien aus dem Kosmos` is independently bibliographic, and later Schneider/Meyl works point specifically to **page 29** for Testatika.

This makes p.29 a concrete target. It is not yet valid to quote its content from later summaries as though the 1989 page had been directly inspected.

Sources:

## 7. The pre-2000 publication archaeology now has four distinct channels

The surviving material should be grouped by provenance rather than by later website:

1. **German/Swiss print witness line:** DVS/Nieper, L.L., Matthey, Hauser, Schneider, raum & zeit, Spuren.
2. **Methernitha institutional line:** Bosshard letter, 1989 Informationsfilm/Sound Track Transcription, later official technical-video/site wording.
3. **Marinov direct-research line:** TWT visits, correspondence, limitations and later interpretations.
4. **US/digital synthesis line:** Kelly/Magnets/IECEC, KeelyNet/BBS, later mirrors.

These channels intersect but are not independent every time they repeat the same statement.

## 8. Current oldest-source mechanism result

After stripping out duplicate republication, the recurring technical cluster remains:

`electrostatic influence / charge separation`
`+ magnetic impulse/timing or magnetic bias`
`+ resonant/electrical conditioning`
`+ capacitive/Leyden storage`.

The source of sustained **real power** still does not converge. The oldest sources provide several different theories, while the direct Methernitha material recovered so far gives a nature/environment conceptual frame but withholds a quantitative coupling mechanism.
