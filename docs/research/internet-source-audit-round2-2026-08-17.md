# Internet source audit — round 2 — 2026-08-17

## Purpose

This round continues the public-Internet crawl after the first audit and its addendum. It records only findings that materially improve provenance, machine separation, source-dependency analysis, acquisition targets or experimental controls. It does **not** treat repetition across websites as independent confirmation.

The existing historical CAD is not silently changed by this audit. A source can change the evidence ledger without becoming a geometry authority.

## 1. Don Kelly 1998: explicit self-correction of an early Testatika depiction

Direct preserved publication:

- Don Kelly, `Dis-Information in the New Energy Field: Specifically on the Swiss M-L Converter (Testatika)`, letter in *New Energy News*, Vol. 6 No. 4, Aug. 1998, pp. 17–18:
  https://www.padrak.com/ine/NEN_6_4_12.html

Kelly states that an Internet depiction showed twin horseshoe magnets and their windings in close interacting proximity to the twin electrostatic discs. He says that depiction came from an early `Magnets`-magazine study and that the early interpretation was later found to be incorrect.

### Consequence

This is a rare **author self-correction**. Any Kelly-derived schematic that places a pair of horseshoe magnets immediately beside the twin discs must therefore be treated as `SUPERSEDED-KELLY`, unless a different direct witness source independently supports the same placement on a named machine.

This correction is narrow:

- it does **not** negate Marinov's direct small-machine magnet observations;
- it does **not** negate Hauser's separately observed large-machine horseshoe modules;
- it does **not** prove that no magnets existed in a Testatika;
- it specifically downgrades the early Kelly depiction as an authority for historical placement/function.

Kelly also rejects radioactive-radium-chloride stories as inconsistent with information he had received. That is useful as a retrospective source-network statement but does not outrank the stronger Baumann→Holzherr statement already preserved in the repository.

## 2. `Magnets` chronology is unresolved — do not manufacture a single source chain

The official *raum&zeit* archive page for `Der Schweizer ML-Konverter` states that Don Kelly described Testatika in `Magnets` in **August 1987** and that *raum&zeit* reproduced his electro-schematic diagram in the original.

- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/der-schweizer-ml-konverter/

Kelly's 1998 letter, however, refers to the erroneous horseshoe-near-disc depiction as coming from an early study in `Magnets` around **1984**.

These statements are not automatically identical. Possibilities include:

1. Kelly misremembered the date in 1998;
2. there were multiple `Magnets` articles/studies;
3. the 1987 article reused an earlier diagram;
4. a later Internet copy mixed more than one Kelly source.

**Decision:** keep two acquisition targets until original issues are obtained:

- Kelly / `Magnets`, August 1987 — article and exact diagram reproduced by *raum&zeit*;
- any Kelly / `Magnets` Testatika item from approximately 1984, if it exists.

The 1991 Kelly/Bailey IECEC reference already identifies another concrete item:

- `More About the Swiss M-L Converter: Pros and Cons`, *Magnets Magazine*, Dec. 1988, pp. 19–26.

No one of these can be substituted for the others without the original pages.

## 3. NEN November 1998: Kelly prototype construction was reported, not successful replication

A preserved *New Energy News*, Vol. 6 No. 6, Nov. 1998 copy reports that Kelly's Space Energy Association was **building a prototype** Swiss M-L converter and describes an intention to extend it toward combined AC/DC household use.

Public preserved issue/index:

- https://www.padrak.com/ine/
- mirror of the Nov. 1998 issue: https://abcdocz.com/doc/178278/volume-6--number-6-issn-1075-0045-november-1998-web-page-...

The same issue says Kelly speculated that new information might permit successful replication. It does **not** provide an independently validated result showing that his prototype worked.

### Consequence

The replication timeline must say:

- 1991: Kelly/Bailey reported known duplication attempts unsuccessful;
- 1998: Kelly/SEA prototype construction reported;
- outcome: **UNKNOWN in sources found so far**.

Do not turn `was building` into `successfully replicated`.

## 4. L. L. / Nieper 17 March 1984 report: exact surviving text strengthens M5a, but its theory remains separate

Public book mirror:

- Hans A. Nieper, *Conversion of Gravity Field Energy / Revolution in Technology, Medicine and Society* (English edition), section `Inspection of the Swiss Tachyon Converter on 17 March 1984`:
  https://www.scribd.com/document/131614540/33883875-Nieper-Conversion-of-Gravity-Field-Energy-Revolution-in-Technology-Medicine-and-Society-1985

The surviving text supports the existing provisional `M5a` entry:

- approx. 110 cm wide × 45 cm deep × 60 cm high;
- approx. 20 kg without clear acrylic cover;
- two acrylic discs approx. 45 cm diameter, close and counterrotating;
- acrylic, light-metal lattice, insulated copper conductors, gold-plated connections;
- `crystal-diode rectifier` terminology in the witness report;
- fan-like 50 lattice/segment positions;
- approx. 60 rpm and magnetic synchronization description;
- separately adjustable lattice pickups;
- central approx. 10-cm rainbow-like disc;
- hand/finger startup followed by claimed continued running;
- lamp/heater demonstration and 300 V / 10 A claim.

The same text contains interpretations about activated air molecules, neutron extraction, resonance and tachyon/gravity energy. These are **not** equivalent to direct component measurements and are not imported as physical mechanism.

### Independence warning

The 17-March-1984 text is extraordinarily similar to the retrospectively published Schneider/Weber 13-March-1984 account. A four-day date difference does not prove two independent observations or two machines. `M5` and `M5a` remain `possible same object / possible source dependence` until original dated notes, photographs, attendee lists or publication history resolve it.

## 5. Nieper/Matthey chronology now has a stronger publication locator

In the same book Nieper states:

- P. H. Matthey saw the converter on 20 Oct. 1984 with several Swiss engineers;
- Nieper says he inspected it on 28 Oct. 1984;
- the book says DVS members inspected the converter on five occasions.

This is valuable visit chronology. It is **not** five independent engineering confirmations because the individual reports, methods and object identity have not all been recovered.

The original P. H. Matthey report remains an acquisition target. A secondary paraphrase must not be used for claimed brushes, plasma vortices or other mechanisms that are absent from the recovered witness text.

## 6. Sarah Tripp documentary: a concrete archive copy exists

Authoritative catalogues provide a new acquisition route for a separate documentary source:

- British Council UK Films database: `Testatika`, Sarah Tripp, listed as 2002, 20 min, Cineworks/Glasgow Media Access Centre:
  https://filmsandfestivals.britishcouncil.org/projects/testatika
- European Film Gateway / National Library of Scotland catalogue: `TESTATIKA`, non-fiction/documentary, listed as 2001, `Cineworks series`:
  https://europeanfilmgateway.eu/

The South London Gallery also described Sarah Tripp's `Testatika` as a 2001 video in a 2002 exhibition.

### Consequence

There is a **catalogue-date conflict (2001/2002)**, likely creation vs database/release year, which must be preserved until the archive record is inspected. More importantly, the National Library of Scotland appears to hold an archival copy. This is now a P1 acquisition target for lawful viewing/frame audit.

No technical detail is imported from the synopsis alone.

## 7. Early web/FTP preservation trail: colossus2 and phoenix.oulu.fi

Bönisch 2003 cites the historical Baylor/BCM `colossus2` Testatika photo archive. Separate early-2000s forum posts preserve the old path:

- `http://colossus2.bcf.bcm.tmc.edu/~wje/free_energy/testatika/`
- `.../testatik.txt`

A Finnish 2003 discussion additionally says its Testatika files had been copied from the already-vanished Oulu FTP tree:

- `ftp://phoenix.oulu.fi/pub/free_energy`

and describes those files as dating from the early 1990s.

This is **archive provenance**, not technical truth. It establishes concrete filenames/paths to search in web/FTP mirrors. Technical claims copied from `testatik.txt` remain secondary unless their upstream source is identified.

## 10. 1989 Einsiedeln proceedings: book-level bibliographic existence strengthened, Testatika pages still unacquired

Multiple bibliographies identify the proceedings:

- P. Engeler, E. Müller, W. Rusterholz (eds.), *Tagungsband Internationaler Kongress für Freie Energie*, Einsiedeln 1989;
- Methernitha, `Informationsfilm Thesta-Distatica: Sound Track Transcription`, ISBN `3-9520025-1-8`.

Independent bibliographic pages list other papers with page identifiers from the same proceedings, strengthening that the volume is a real, structured publication rather than merely an Internet citation. The actual Methernitha transcript pages have not yet been recovered in this crawl.

The target remains OPEN.
