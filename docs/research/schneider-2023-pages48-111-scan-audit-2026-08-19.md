## 1. Pages 48–50 — autonomous supply and the radioactivity question

The section continues the 34-engineer / demonstration discussion and frames the Testatika as an autonomous source. Printed p.50 carries the heading **`Keine radioaktive Quelle`**.

Research consequence:

- this is useful for the historical genealogy of the radioactivity question;
- the pages do **not** supply a documented radiation-survey protocol, instrument, count-rate series or closed technical assay;
- therefore the heading must not be upgraded to a measured exclusion of all radioactive material;
- Camus/radium remains a separate later hypothesis line and does not enter the canonical Swiss-Testatika mechanism.

Status: **secondary/witness-summary lead; no quantitative radiation result**.

## 2. Pages 51–57 — Frolov section and the `1 kΩ between lamellae` claim

The Frolov discussion contains several high-output and field/induction interpretations that are not presented with a closed measurement chain. They remain secondary explanatory material.

### Important printed provenance on p.56

The book states under `Konstruktionshinweis` that Baumann said each lamella of the disk was connected to its neighbouring lamella through a **1000-ohm resistor**, producing a circular current path.

This is valuable because it provides a concrete printed locator for the late `1 kΩ` claim.

It does **not** upgrade the claim to the M2 baseline. The repository already holds stronger direct Marinov correspondence for the photographed small-machine family saying that the rotor wires were **`connected to nothing`**. Consequently:

- `1 kΩ neighbour ring`: keep as **late relayed attribution / hypothesis lead**;
- isolated floating rotor conductors: remain the preferred small-machine baseline;
- possible reconciliation by machine variant is allowed, but must be source-bridged rather than assumed.

This scan therefore improves **provenance of the conflict**, not confidence in the resistor-ring topology.

## 3. Pages 58–61 — Kaspar/Karlen and P. H. Matthey

### Pages 59–60 — Kaspar/Karlen

The witness material contains claims about useful power and autonomous operation, but no synchronized closed input/output energy measurement is reproduced in these pages. Retain as witness/secondary demonstration material only.

### Page 61 — high-value Matthey relay

Printed p.61 is one of the most important pages in this scan set. Under **`Besuch von Dipl.-Ing. P.H. Matthey bei der Methernitha`**, the book says the report is found through Stefan Marinov's *The Thorny Way of Truth*, Band V, and describes Matthey as having been invited by Paul Baumann in 1984.

The page relays the following claims:

- an approximately **one-hour demonstration**;
- the device allegedly supplied **several kilowatts of usable current** during that demonstration;
- Baumann's startup explanation is described as a **charge phase** (`Ladungsphase` / charging phase);
- two comparatively slowly rotating disks are involved in that start description;
- high voltage is said to arise and then to be transformed/amplified or conditioned into useful output;
- operation is described as **weather-dependent**, with more energy in thunderstorm-like conditions;
- the device is said to have had no mains/socket connection and no battery;
- Matthey is relayed as saying he had convinced himself that no fraud was involved.

### Evidence classification

This page is **S2 / later relay of an older Matthey report**, not the still-missing original 1984 report.

The most important new research value is the conjunction:

`startup = charge phase`

`+ slow rotating disks`

`+ claimed HV formation/conditioning`

`+ claimed weather sensitivity`.

That conjunction is compatible with the project's charge-state / environmental-boundary research questions, but it does not prove the energy source or the claimed kilowatt output.

The original P. H. Matthey report remains a top acquisition target.

## 6. Pages 76–84 — Marinov: technically important, but mostly already primary-secure elsewhere

This is the most technically useful block after p.61, but the repository already contains stronger direct Marinov scans/correspondence. The 2023 book therefore acts mainly as a readable synthesis/cross-check.

### Pages 78–79 — Marinov electrostatic-motor test

The book reproduces the familiar high-voltage electrostatic-motor experiment and the claimed large ratio between inferred mechanical output and electrical input.

Repository guardrail remains:

- the approximately **25-kV** motor experiment and Marinov's reported factor around **13.3** are historically important;
- they do **not** constitute a closed simultaneous COP measurement;
- current repo metrology analysis therefore does not treat the factor as verified over-unity.

### Pages 80–83 — small-machine architecture and startup clues

The block reiterates several high-value small-machine constraints already secured from direct Marinov material:

- electrostatic rather than ordinary motor drive in the described small-machine configuration;
- distinct drive/collection roles;
- the drive capacitors require an initial charge and continued replenishment because charge is lost;
- dry-air startup needs only a few pushes, with stronger humidity dependence;
- East-West startup orientation is relayed in the Baumann→Marinov line, while the already-running machine could later be moved/reoriented;
- roughly one revolution per second in the relevant small-machine account;
- a nearby/rear metal plate can strongly disturb or stop operation.

### Research consequence

The p.82 recharge statement is especially important conceptually because it isolates the exact unresolved problem:

`stored/drive charge is consumed or leaked -> it must be regenerated each cycle`.

This supports the **question** behind the project's `cyclic charge-state regeneration` model, but does not identify the missing real-power reservoir.

No evidence-weight increase is assigned where stronger primary Marinov scans already exist.

# Net effect on the current research model

## What is genuinely added or sharpened

### A. Matthey p.61 becomes a high-priority secondary relay

The strongest new lead is the 1984 Matthey chain as reproduced on p.61:

`one-hour demonstration`
`+ several-kW claim`
`+ startup described as charging phase`
`+ slow disks / HV formation`
`+ claimed weather dependence`
`+ no visible/declared socket or battery`.

This does not close the energy balance. It does, however, make recovery of the **original Matthey report** even more valuable.

### B. The `1 kΩ` resistor-ring claim gets a concrete printed locator

Printed p.56 gives an explicit later Baumann attribution. The claim remains in conflict with the stronger direct small-machine Marinov statement `connected to nothing`. This is a provenance improvement, not a topology upgrade.

### C. The recharge problem is independently highlighted in the later synthesis

The Marinov block restates that the drive capacitors lose charge and require replenishment. That aligns with the project's focus on **charge-state regeneration**, while the actual bulk source remains unresolved.

## What does NOT change

- no verified over-unity or closed kilowatt power balance;
- no verified radioactive/radium source;
- no authentic complete Testatika circuit;
- no reason to import Kelly/Potter/Camus/Manghin reconstructions wholesale into M2;
- no reason to override direct Marinov floating-rotor evidence with the later `1 kΩ` claim;
- no identified source of sustained real power.

The primary energy source remains **UNKNOWN**.
