# 1984 witness-source independence audit

## Purpose

Two early published Testatika witness lines are now accessible online:

- **M5** — Inge Schneider / Hans Weber visit dated 13 March 1984, known through Schneider's later-published account of her 1994 brochure and the 2004 seminar programme;
- **M5a** — witness report dated 17 March 1984, reproduced in Hans Nieper's 1985 book and signed only `L. L., Rorschach, Switzerland`.

Their physical descriptions are so similar that they must **not** be counted as independent corroboration until textual/source dependence is resolved.

## Why this matters

The two reports may represent:

1. the **same physical machine** shown on two nearby dates;
2. two witness groups whose later publications reused a common descriptive text;
3. one original report reused/edited by another publication;
4. genuinely independent descriptions of the same object.

Current public evidence does not distinguish those possibilities.

Therefore:

- matching dimensions are **not multiplied as independent measurements**;
- identical 300 V / 10 A claims are treated as demonstration/source claims, not separate measurements;
- M5a-specific details such as ~45-cm discs, 50 grid positions and ~60 rpm may guide a separate research variant, but must not silently modify M5 or M6a;
- acquisition of Schneider's original 1994 brochure, the original L. L. report and any 1984 video/notes is high priority.

## Physical observation versus theory

The L. L. report interleaves geometry with theoretical language about air activation, neutron extraction, gravity/tachyon fields and resonance. The latter are **interpretations**, not observations established by the described demonstration.

Likewise, Schneider/Weber's conclusion that the demonstration contradicted energy conservation is a witness conclusion. Looking underneath and seeing no battery does not bound all stored energy, hidden feeds, prior charging or measurement error.

## Current CAD decision

No existing M6a CAD is changed from these reports. M5 and M5a remain documentary machine families until calibrated photographs/drawings or original construction records justify a dedicated CAD build.
