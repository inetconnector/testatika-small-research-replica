# Stefan Hartmann / Methernitha / 8-mm film — provenance update 2026-08-19

**Status:** additive correction and source upgrade. This note does not prove Testatika net output and does not identify an unknown camera operator by inference.

## 1. Direct Hartmann self-report of a Methernitha visit

An early Stefan Hartmann text, preserved by the Totse mirror and dated January/February 1991, contains the explicit first-person statement that he personally visited Methernitha. The same text says his Testatika animation was made from digitized pictures from a videotape sold by Methernitha.

Source:
- https://newtotse.com/oldtotse/en/fringe/free_energy/statika1.html

Evidence class:
- **H1-M / DIRECT-AUTHOR MIRROR** for Hartmann's own claim that he visited Methernitha;
- **not** a calibrated Testatika power measurement;
- the preserved page is a mirror, not the original 1991 server.

This upgrades the older `Rimstar -> Hartmann visited Methernitha` item from being the only visit lead. Rimstar remains a secondary source for the separate claim that Hartmann was told that the secret was in the crystals.

## 2. 1999 Holzherr demonstration and Hartmann's actual role

The surviving 1999 correspondence is explicit about roles:

- Hans Holzherr was present at the Methernitha demonstration with more than 30 technicians/engineers;
- the 50-cm machine was already running when the group entered and reportedly remained running during the roughly 1.5-hour visit;
- the nominal 1000-W lamp was connected for approximately 10 seconds;
- Holzherr refers to the corresponding sequence on the `Testatika film`;
- Hartmann was the recipient/questioner, translator and distributor of Holzherr's report;
- Hartmann asked permission to put Holzherr's picture on the overunity.com server and to translate/post the emails to Free Energy lists.

Sources:
- https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/report99.htm
- https://www.novakcorp.com/energy/experiments/tesnews.htm
- https://www.rexresearch.com/testatik/testart.htm

Evidence consequence:

`1999 Methernitha demo -> Holzherr eyewitness -> Hartmann questions/translates/distributes`

must remain separate from Hartmann's own earlier/later visit(s).

## 3. Independent 8-mm-film provenance: Cabinet Magazine 2006

Nick Laessing's 2006 article in *Cabinet Magazine* reproduces a still described as coming from an **8-mm film** of the Testatika while the machine is shown powering a 1000-W bulb. The image credit is explicitly **Courtesy Stefan Hartmann**.

The article further states that the surviving pictures came from an 8-mm film made during a 1999 demonstration for a group of about thirty technicians and engineers lasting about ninety minutes.

Source:
- https://www.cabinetmagazine.org/issues/21/laessing.php

Evidence consequence:

- Hartmann demonstrably had access to, supplied, or controlled a copy/still from the 8-mm film by 2006;
- this is strong **media-provenance** evidence linking Hartmann to dissemination of the film;
- it is **not evidence that Hartmann himself operated the 8-mm camera**;
- the 1999 film event is better tied to the thirty-person demonstration than to Hartmann's separate personal Methernitha visit.

## 4. Do not merge the 1999 8-mm film with the Cathomen workshop video

The repository contains a different video lineage, `testa01.ram` / `testa02.ram`, from the 2001 workshop material. Embedded metadata for `testa01.ram` says **`Dieter Dienst speaking with Luzi Cathomen`**, while `testa02.ram` identifies Dieter Dienst as author.

Therefore the older project inference `D = Stefan Hartmann` in public Cathomen transcripts is now **CONFLICT / unresolved** and must not be used as a settled identity.

Correct rule:

- `D` in the transcript = German main questioner, identity unresolved from the transcript itself;
- embedded archive metadata points to **Dieter Dienst** and is the strongest current named provenance clue;
- Hartmann's documented Methernitha visit and media-distribution role do **not** by themselves identify him as `D`;
- no Cathomen quote should be re-labelled as a Hartmann question unless an explicit timestamp/person bridge is found.

This correction supersedes any earlier project wording that treated Hartmann as the probable or established main interviewer.

## 5. What is established about Hartmann and the film

### Supported

1. Hartmann directly wrote that he had personally visited Methernitha (early-1991 mirrored text).
2. Hartmann was the 1999 recipient/questioner/translator/distributor of Holzherr's eyewitness report.
3. Holzherr explicitly refers to a `Testatika film` associated with the lamp demonstration.
4. *Cabinet Magazine* in 2006 credits an 8-mm Testatika-film still `Courtesy Stefan Hartmann`.
5. *Cabinet* links that film to the 1999 ~30-technician/engineer, ~90-minute demonstration.
6. Historical Overunity.com paths hosted/distributed Testatika media; this supports Hartmann's broader media-archive role because he owned/administered the platform, but each file still needs its own authorship chain.

### Not established

1. Hartmann personally filmed the known 1999 8-mm footage.
2. Hartmann was one of the approximately thirty 1999 attendees.
3. The known 1999 8-mm film and the 2001 Cathomen `testa01/testa02` workshop recording are the same recording.
4. Hartmann is the `D` interviewer in the Cathomen transcript.
5. The 1000-W lamp label is a closed calorimetric/electrical 1000-W measurement.
6. A later YouTube upload alone proves original camera authorship.
