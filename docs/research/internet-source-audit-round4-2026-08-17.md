# Internet source audit — round 4 — 2026-08-17

## Scope

Round 4 concentrates on chronology/provenance traps that can easily become false engineering facts when repeated online. In particular it audits:

- the late `Marinov received a 100-W Testatika in 1997` story;
- conflicting 1999 engineer-demonstration dates/counts;
- the 2004 Zurich Testatika seminar and surviving video lead;
- the early Nieper/1980 attribution;
- early Testatika construction-year conflicts;
- Hauser's publication-transmission chain;
- exact `raum&zeit` 40/1989 publication metadata;
- the Nuetec higher-resolution film and companion-document acquisition route.

No claim is promoted merely because multiple websites copy the same text.

## 1. The `1997 100-W Testatika gift to Marinov` story is not supported by the located source chain

### Late source making the strong claim

Alexander Frolov's 2021 book *New Sources of Energy* says that in 1997 Stefan Marinov `received a small 100 W Testatika generator` and invited Prof. Lev Sapogin to study it.

Public mirrors:

- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356969854_New_Sources_of_Energy_English_version
- https://studylib.net/doc/25657510/frolov--alexander.-new-sources-of-energy--2021--with-173-...

No contemporaneous 1997 transfer record, photograph with transfer provenance, letter from Marinov/Baumann, inventory record, or primary Sapogin document was located in this crawl.

### Earlier Watson transmission is much weaker/different

Mike Watson's 21-Jul-2001 email says Baumann unexpectedly `gave Marinov one of the small machines to play with`. In the same message Watson says:

- the observations were made at Linden;
- Marinov had no quantitative test equipment available;
- Marinov estimated rotor mechanical power below about 100 mW;
- he estimated electrical output around 100 W from resistor heating.

Public preserved mirrors:

- https://www.robkalmeijer.nl/techniek/experiments/testakica/index.html
- https://www.novakcorp.com/energy/experiments/bswiss.htm

The phrase `gave ... to play with` does **not** establish permanent ownership or that the machine left Linden.

### Marinov's own source separates the estimates

Marinov's own 1989 Testatika text reports the same kind of qualitative estimates: very small mechanical wheel power by feel/experience and roughly 100-W-scale resistor heating, without calibrated power metrology.

### Decision

Classify:

> `Marinov received a 100-W Testatika generator in 1997` = **LATE SECONDARY / UNSUPPORTED AS A HISTORICAL TRANSFER / LIKELY CONFLATION**.

The likely conflation is between:

1. Watson's later `gave ... to play with` wording;
2. Marinov's rough ~100-W resistor-heating estimate;
3. a later assumption that `gave` meant a permanent gift and `100 W` was a device rating.

This remains a hypothesis about how the story evolved; the underlying strong claim itself is not verified.

Do **not** delete the Frolov/Sapogin claim from historical-source tracking. Preserve it as a contradiction/source-evolution example.

## 2. 1999 engineer demonstration: date and participant count remain CONFLICT

### 2004 seminar programme

A surviving programme for the Jupiter-Verlag / Institut für Bionik seminar `Das Geheimnis der Testatika`, Zurich, 13-Mar-2004, lists:

- `Experimente vor 34 Ingenieuren am 5. Juni 1999`;
- `Eindrücke von einer Demo vor Altherren des Technikums Burgdorf`.

Source:

- https://guns.connect.fi/innoplaza/energy/conference/Schneider/Testatika.html

### 2011 NET-Journal retrospective

The Schneider/NET-Journal obituary article says instead that on **4-Aug-1999** **30 Swiss engineers** saw several Testatika machines operating and studied/tried to reproduce a basic model.

Source:

- https://www.yumpu.com/de/document/view/4114787/paul-baumann-erbauer-der-energiemaschine-testatika-93jahrig-

### Chronology warning

The existing Holzherr/Hartmann correspondence lineage contains early-August-1999 email/translation dates. Therefore a later `4 August` visit date may be publication/memory conflation, but that cannot be asserted without the contemporaneous visit document.

### Decision

Retain all of the following without normalization:

- `5-Jun-1999 / 34 engineers / Burgdorf alumni` — 2004 seminar-programme statement;
- `4-Aug-1999 / 30 Swiss engineers` — 2011 Schneider retrospective;
- Holzherr/Hartmann early-August email/translation chronology — separate transmission metadata.

Status: **DATE/COUNT CONFLICT**.

What would resolve it: contemporaneous invitation, attendee list, dated photograph/video, signed report, diary/letter, or original event programme.

## 3. The 2004 Zurich seminar is a strong provenance node, not primary Testatika evidence

The surviving programme fixes the event:

- Saturday 13-Mar-2004, Technopark Zurich;
- Hans Weber presentation about the 13-Mar-1984 encounter, explicitly `mit Video`;
- 1999-engineer-demo presentation;
- Weber theoretical model `Ladungsaustausch über Polymerketten bei Luftionisation`;

This is valuable because it timestamps **who advocated which theory in 2004**. It does not make those theories Methernitha engineering facts.

Source page:

The video itself has not yet been recovered/audited in this crawl. It is now a P0/P1 acquisition target because it may contain Weber's own retelling, the referenced 1984 video excerpt, and Bönisch/Schneider contextual material.

## 4. Weber's polymer-chain / air-ionization model is a dated later hypothesis

The 2004 programme explicitly titles Weber's explanatory contribution:

`Ladungsaustausch über Polymerketten bei Luftionisation`.

Correct classification:

- high confidence that **Weber presented this model in 2004**;
- low/unknown confidence that this describes the historical Testatika mechanism;
- it must not be merged with Baumann statements, Hauser atmospheric recollections, or Hartmann electret/air-ion hypotheses as if they were one independent mechanism.

It can motivate controlled material/ionization experiments only.

## 5. 2010 Methernitha reply: recipient-published provenance upgraded

The 2011 NET-Journal article says its editors asked Methernitha in 2010 about continued Testatika development and reproduces the reply. In substance the reply says:

- the alternative-energy research group no longer exists;
- the Thestatika can no longer be shown;
- Internet entries about the Thestatika were made without their knowledge;
- they believe the machine helped motivate people to search for renewable-energy alternatives.

This is stronger provenance than an anonymous/forum quotation because it is published by the stated correspondence recipient in a dated journal article.

Classification:

`RECIPIENT-PUBLISHED INSTITUTIONAL CORRESPONDENCE`.

Still not equivalent to possessing the original message/header or an archived official Methernitha webpage. Original correspondence remains an acquisition target.

## 7. Early Nieper/Testatika chronology: the `November 1980` date is not verified

The Nuetec Testatika page introduces a sentence attributed to Marinov saying that the first man who admitted to him having seen/tested Testatika was Dr. Hans Nieper, followed by the parenthetical:

`(an seiner Konferenz in Hannover; WR: im November 1980)`.

Source:

- https://nuetec-forschung.de/Thesta-Distatika/Testatika.html

The marker `WR:` visibly identifies an editor/translator annotation. Public sources independently verify that a Hans Nieper gravity-field-energy symposium occurred in Hannover in Nov. 1980, but this does **not** by itself establish that Nieper had already seen/tested Testatika by then.

Targeted searches have not located a source-language Marinov passage or contemporaneous 1980 Nieper Testatika report fixing that date.

Decision:

- `Marinov later said Nieper was the first person who told him he had seen/tested Testatika` = **TRANSLATION/EDITOR-MEDIATED LEAD, acquire original**;
- `this disclosure occurred at the Nov-1980 Hannover conference` = **EDITOR ATTRIBUTION / UNVERIFIED**;
- do not set `first Testatika witness = 1980` in the canonical timeline.

## 8. Construction-year conflict: 1977 versus 1978 and other retrospective dates

### Marinov 1996 retrospective

A 1996 NEN article supplied by Marinov says the first functioning perpetual-motion machine he saw was Testatika and describes it as constructed by Paul Baumann in **1977**.

Source:

- https://www.padrak.com/ine/FINSRUD1.html

### Marinov 1989/Italian author text

Marinov's Testatika article says Baumann built the **two small one-disc machines photographed with Marinov in 1978**, in prison. It also describes an earlier lever/Zamboni-like version preceding the rotating-disc form.

Public mirror:

- https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/l-energia-che-viene-dal-niente-stefan-marinov/2617485

### Decision

These dates need not describe the same milestone:

- earlier non-disc/Zamboni-like prototype;
- first functioning Testatika family object;
- the two photographed one-disc machines.

Do not normalize `1977` and `1978` into one build date without original construction records. Treat as **MILESTONE/RETROSPECTIVE-DATE CONFLICT**.

Secondary Pajak timelines are not used to resolve it because Pajak explicitly mixes reconstruction, reports and his own theoretical framework.
