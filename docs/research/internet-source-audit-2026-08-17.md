# Internet source audit — 2026-08-17

## Scope and method

This audit searches public Internet material for Testatika / Thesta-Distatica information that is missing from the repository or presently misclassified. It is **not** a list of everything repeated online. A search hit is only a lead until its authorship, date, machine identity and transmission chain are separated.

Evidence handling in this audit:

- **P0/P1** — primary-author or direct-observer material, including later mirrors of an identifiable original text; mirror/translation status is retained.
- **W1/H1/H2** — witness transmission or hearsay; the chain is stated explicitly.
- **S2/I1** — secondary compilation or interpretation; useful for source discovery/hypothesis formation only.
- **CONTROL** — independent conventional experiment or replication attempt; it does not establish historical Testatika construction.
- A historical load claim is never converted into a net-energy measurement without a closed input/output/storage balance.

The audit deliberately keeps machine variants separate. New information is not copied into M2 or M6a simply because it is described as “Testatika”.

## 1. Stefan Marinov — direct-author material materially changes the M2 evidence ledger

### 1.1 Direct 1989 Testatika article / TWT-V-related material

A public mirror/translation of Marinov's 1989 Testatika article gives several statements that are stronger than later Mike-Watson retellings. The important distinction is that the **author is Marinov**, while the online German copy is a later translation/mirror.

Corroborating author mirror:

- Stefan Marinov, *L'energia che viene dal niente* (Italian author text, later online mirror):
  https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/l-energia-che-viene-dal-niente-stefan-marinov/2617485

Source-level consequences:

1. **Marinov says he did not dismantle Testatika and did not possess technical drawings.** Drawings in his publications came from Albert Hauser, who examined a machine, and Don Kelly, who had only seen photographs. This directly limits the evidentiary weight of Kelly geometry.
2. **The first small machines are about 20 cm; the two-disc machines are about 50 cm.** Marinov additionally states that 100-cm and 200-cm disc machines were under construction at the time.
3. Marinov's two-bus / small-C-high-V versus large-C-low-V explanation is his own interpretation. It is useful as a falsifiable model but must not be relabelled as recovered original wiring.
4. His load-reaction observations and hand-estimated power remain historical observations/estimates, not a calorimetric or closed energy balance.

### 1.2 East–West startup is stronger than previously classified

Marinov's own account states that Baumann told him the axis of the small machine had to be East–West for startup, and that the machine was positioned that way to start. Marinov then reports a separate observation: **after startup the running machine could be moved/tilted without stopping**.

Therefore the repository must distinguish:

- `E-W required for small-machine startup` = **SOURCE-STATED, Baumann → Marinov**, not H2;
- `post-start orientation independence` = **Marinov OBSERVED**;
- `geomagnetic cause` = **UNKNOWN/HYPOTHESIS**, not established by either statement.

### 1.3 Dry-air push count and restart-memory observation

Marinov reports that in dry air approximately **3–4 initial finger pushes** were enough, while higher humidity required more. He also reports that after stopping with a rear metal plate, subsequent starts were easier than the first.

This is important experimentally because it separates at least three variables that Internet summaries often collapse:

- humidity/leakage;
- startup azimuth;
- history/preconditioning state.

The correct experiment is therefore randomized and state-controlled, not simply “point East–West and see whether it spins”.

### 1.4 Rear metal plate stop is direct-observer evidence

Marinov reports that bringing a large metal plate behind the small machine stopped rotation and removed the static/rest torque; restart then required new pushes. This must be upgraded from a later-transmission/H2 item to **direct Marinov observation**.

It does **not** establish why the plate stopped the machine. Plausible conventional variables include capacitance, field boundary conditions, leakage, grounding and induced charge. Those must be separated experimentally.

### 1.5 Small-machine running speed and direction

Marinov reports roughly **one revolution per second (~60 rpm)** and one rotational direction in his viewing convention. Direction wording is retained with a viewing-direction caveat; ~60 rpm is a model/source-specific observation, not a universal Testatika speed.

### 1.6 Subjective power estimates remain non-metrological

Marinov estimated the small wheel's mechanical power by touch at below about 100 mW and judged resistor heating at roughly 100 W or more. His own text makes clear that these are not instrumented, closed energy measurements. They are retained for historical completeness only.

## 2. Marinov TWT Part VII — new two-disc sector architecture evidence

Primary-author book mirror:

- Stefan Marinov, *The Thorny Way of Truth, Part VII*, first published 1990:
  https://www.scribd.com/document/117713319/The-Thorny-Way-of-Truth-Part7-Marinov

In the Testatika additional note Marinov writes that:

- thick sectors occur **only on one wheel**;
- electrically connected **GRID sectors are on both sides** of that Plexiglas wheel;
- thin sectors on the other wheel occur only on its **one external side**;
- surfaces of the slightly magnetized sectors are covered by insulating spray.

This is important primary-author evidence, but the exact medium/large machine identity is not sufficiently locked to the present M6a Hauser CAD. It is therefore recorded as a **large/two-disc family constraint and configuration conflict**, not silently retrofitted into M6a V1.

### 2.1 Marinov's own conventional control did not close

In the same volume Marinov describes a mechanically/electrically coupled Wimshurst generator and electrostatic motor. For equivalent electrical output/input he reports the generator's braking mechanical power as about **4–5 times larger** than the motor's driving mechanical power. Whatever Marinov expected theoretically, his own constructed conventional loop therefore did **not** self-close.

This is valuable negative/control evidence and belongs in the experiment methodology: electrostatic motor/generator intuition cannot substitute for full torque/power measurement.

## 3. Large-machine variant conflict: Marinov versus Hauser

Marinov reports seeing parts of a large machine under construction and describes it as approximately a 2:1 copy of the medium machine. In his account:

- large/medium sectors are perforated metal;
- Baumann told him the large sectors used a special Fe-Ni alloy and were lightly magnetized;
- the large machine had horseshoe magnets of roughly 30 cm scale;
- when he saw large-machine cylindrical “capacitive transformers” open, he interpreted them as simple capacitors with a cylindrical outer electrode and a thick copper-wire coil as inner electrode.

This is not safely mergeable with Hauser's 1988 M6a description of three concentric grids + acrylic separators + central magnet tube + bifilar winding. Both may describe different machines, construction stages, or interpretations.

**Decision:** introduce `M6c` as the **Marinov-1989 large-under-construction / 2:1-medium-copy family** until a source bridge proves identity with M6a or M6b. No current M6a CAD is overwritten by this audit.

## 4. One-metre, two-metre and tandem workshop families

A preserved transcript of the amateur workshop video, attributed in the archive/video line to Luzi Cathomen speaking with two German visitors, explicitly distinguishes:

- a **2-metre** machine;
- a **1-metre** machine;
- a **double converter**;
- a **tandem** arrangement;
- a stated approximate rotational rate of 60 rpm in the discussed context;
- Leyden-jar/condenser stages and a deliberately undisclosed upper conditioning stage.

Mirror:

- https://www.robkalmeijer.nl/techniek/experiments/testakica/index.html

The English text is visibly machine-translated/garbled in places. It is therefore used for **object taxonomy and source acquisition**, not for precise electrical wording where the original German audio/transcript is required.

Separate machine IDs are warranted:

- `M8` — ~1 m unfinished/large-scale family;
- `M9` — ~2 m unfinished/large-scale family (“Elephant” in later secondary nomenclature);
- `M10` — tandem/double-converter workshop family.

Reported kW numbers remain **operator claims**, not measured net output.

## 9. Weber/Schneider line — event provenance, not yet an engineering baseline

Public seminar/catalogue material points to a Testatika encounter/demonstration on **13 March 1984** involving Hans Weber and Inge Schneider/Schönthal, plus a later 5 June 1999 event involving a group described as 34 engineers/former Burgdorf Technikum students.

These pages are useful for **event and acquisition provenance**, but their retrospective output claims are not treated as closed energy measurements. The original 1984 notes/video and the 1999 engineer-group documentation remain acquisition targets.

## 11. Conventional/replication controls found online

### 11.1 Sven Bönisch 2003

Sven Bönisch, *Electrostatic Discharge Power Transformation — An Approach to Understand the Working Principles of the “Thestatika” Free Energy Device*, ELEKTRIE 5–8/2003, ISSN 0013-5399:

- public mirror: https://studfile.net/preview/8197487/

Bönisch explicitly describes his system as based on known electrodynamics and says it should not exceed 100% efficiency. His measured conventional apparatus produced milliwatt-scale output; his paper concludes that the tested transformation system fulfilled energy conservation and that no over-unity effect was detected.

This is **CONTROL evidence**, not a source for hidden Testatika wiring. It is valuable because it supplies a conventional electrostatic/ESD comparison and shows how resonant HV structures can produce impressive waveforms without implying energy creation.

### 11.2 Rimstar / Steven Dufresne instrumentation warning

Rimstar's Testatika experiments document an important measurement failure mode: an early floating-source experiment was invalidated because the oscilloscope connection unintentionally grounded the source. Later testing corrected the floating measurement configuration.

Experiment index:

- https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/

This is not historical evidence, but it is a strong **metrology rule** for this project: every oscilloscope, probe, DAQ, USB earth and motor-controller earth path must be included in the node map before interpreting a Testatika-like floating electrostatic measurement.

## 12. Secondary/back-engineering sources explicitly downgraded

### Don Kelly

Marinov's own Italian author text says Don Kelly had **only seen photographs**. Kelly therefore cannot serve as a direct-observer geometry authority. Kelly remains S2/source-discovery material.

### Paul E. Potter

Potter's “Back-Engineered” material is explicitly a reconstruction/interpretation and contains many speculative mechanisms. It remains useful for testable hypotheses and source leads but cannot define original wiring/material/function.

### Hartmann later hypotheses

Hartmann's electret, air-ion, radioactive-mineral and negative-resistance ideas remain Hartmann hypotheses at their respective dates. The presence of a mirror is evidence that Hartmann proposed them, not evidence that Methernitha used those mechanisms.

## 13. Corrections made by this audit

The following repository statements should be considered corrected after this audit:

1. M2 East–West startup is **not merely H2**; it is Baumann→Marinov source-stated, while post-start orientation independence is Marinov-observed.
2. M2 rear-metal-plate stop is **direct Marinov observation**, not merely later transmission.
3. M2 startup history includes dry-air 3–4-push behavior, humidity sensitivity and easier later restarts.
4. M2 ~60 rpm is a direct Marinov model-specific observation.
5. TWT VII adds a two-disc thick-grid/thin-sector asymmetry and insulating sector spray, but its exact M6a identity is unresolved.
6. Kelly drawings are explicitly limited by Marinov's statement that Kelly only saw photographs.
7. Marinov's own Wimshurst→electrostatic-motor control failed to close energetically, strengthening the requirement for measured torque/power balance.
8. M6 has at least one source-conflict family (`M6c`) that must not be silently collapsed into Hauser M6a.
9. 1 m, 2 m and tandem workshop objects deserve separate machine IDs instead of being dumped into `MX`.

## 14. What this crawl did **not** solve

No reliable public source found in this round supplies:

- the complete authentic M2 node-to-node schematic;
- exact M2 through-disc wire route;
- exact M2 pot capacitance/polarity/turn count;
- crystal material and measured I–V curve;
- exact original M6a cylinder node map;
- a closed, independently replicated historical net-energy balance;
- a provenance-secure explanation of the claimed energy source.

Those fields remain UNKNOWN. The absence of a public source is not evidence that a particular speculative circuit is correct.
