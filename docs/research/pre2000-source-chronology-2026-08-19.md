# Testatika / Methernitha — systematic pre-2000 source chronology

**Audit date:** 2026-08-19  
**Scope:** earliest recoverable Testatika / Swiss M-L Converter / Thesta-Distatica records before 2000.  
**Purpose:** separate event date, publication date, eyewitness statement, operator/institutional statement, later interpretation, reprint, and unresolved source lead.

## Method

This chronology applies four hard rules:

1. **Event date is not publication date.** A 1984 visit described in a 1994 book is a 1994 retrospective source about a 1984 event.
2. **Observation is not causal explanation.** A visitor may correctly report geometry or a demonstration while incorrectly explaining the energy source.
3. **Reprints and mirrors are not independent witnesses.** A claim copied through Nieper -> Kelly -> web mirrors counts as one genealogy unless a genuinely independent source is found.
4. **Operator/institutional statements remain separate from visitor theories.** Methernitha/Baumann/Bosshard, Marinov, Hauser, Kelly, Schneider, Hartmann and later Internet authors are distinct sources.

The goal is not to decide whether claimed net power was real. The goal is to recover what was actually said, by whom, when, and how close each surviving record is to the event.

## Chronology

### 1983 — DVS inspections: earliest located event lead, original report still missing

Hans A. Nieper's 1985 book states that selected members of the German Association for Gravity Field Energy / DVS inspected the Swiss converter in **1983 and 1984**.

Current status:
- **H2 / RELAY** for the existence of a 1983 inspection;
- no original 1983 DVS field report, visitor list, photographs or measurement sheets recovered in this audit;
- do not use `1983` as a source for specific geometry or mechanism until an original report is acquired.

Surviving source genealogy:
`1983 inspection(s) -> Nieper 1985 retrospective statement -> later Kelly/web repetitions`.

Source mirror of Nieper 1985:
- https://www.scribd.com/document/131614540/33883875-Nieper-Conversion-of-Gravity-Field-Energy-Revolution-in-Technology-Medicine-and-Society-1985

### 1984-03-13 — Inge Schönthal/Schneider + Hans Weber visit; detailed surviving account is later

Current Jupiter-Verlag material dates the visit of Inge Schönthal (later Schneider) and Dr. Hans Weber to **13 March 1984**.

However, the detailed prose account currently located is cited to Inge Schneider's **1994** book `Neue Technologien zur Freien Energie`. Therefore:

- event date: **1984-03-13**;
- detailed source date currently secured: **1994 retrospective**;
- source class: **W1-retrospective**, not a contemporaneous 1984 publication;
- any claimed direct Baumann wording in the 1994 narrative remains retrospective unless an earlier notebook/report is recovered.

Sources:
- https://www.jupiter-verlag.ch/shop/auswahl_neu.php?fromMain=j
- bibliographic confirmation of `Neue Technologien zur Freien Energie`, Jupiter-Verlag 1994 in later Schneider bibliography.

Acquisition target: original 1984 notes, correspondence, photographs, instrument readings or a pre-1994 publication by Schneider/Weber.

### 1984-03-17 — L. L., Rorschach eyewitness report, preserved in Nieper 1985

Nieper's 1985 book reproduces a report headed **`Inspection of the Swiss Tachyon Converter on 17 March 1984`**, signed `L. L., Rorschach, Switzerland`.

High-value reported details include:
- acrylic/plexiglass structure, light-metal lattice and insulated copper wiring;
- two counterrotating ~45-cm discs;
- fan-like set of about 50 lattice electrodes;
- about **60 rpm**;
- disc speed described as **synchronized by magnetic impulses**;
- strong horseshoe magnets described as parts of **electrical resonance circuits**;
- hand start followed by claimed self-synchronization;
- Leyden-bottle storage and load demonstrations.

Evidence caution:
- the physical description and what the witness says happened are **W1** witness evidence;
- `the secret lies...` and the resonance-circuit causal wording may be the witness's own technical interpretation unless an original operator quotation is recovered;
- output values and indefinite-running statements are not a closed energy balance.

Source:
- Nieper 1985 mirror above, section `Inspection of the Swiss Tachyon Converter on March 17, 1984`.

### 1984-10-20 — P. H. Matthey visit/report, currently preserved through Nieper

Nieper writes that Swiss electronics engineer **P. H. Matthey**, a DVS member, saw the converter on **20 October 1984** with several other Swiss engineers. Nieper quotes Matthey as finding the absence of ordinary primary propulsion incompatible with conventional teaching physics.

Status:
- visit provenance: **H1/H2 via Nieper**, medium-high;
- exact Matthey report: **not yet independently acquired**;
- Nieper's `gravity stressing field` interpretation must not be silently converted into Matthey or Methernitha operator doctrine.

Acquisition target: original P. H. Matthey report and DVS issue/circular in which it first appeared.

### 1984-10-28 — Hans A. Nieper own inspection, published 1985

Nieper states in first person that he inspected the converter on **28 October 1984**. His book then interprets the machine through his `gravity stressing / tachyon field` framework.

Status:
- **H1 direct-author self-report** for Nieper's own visit;
- **HYPOTHESIS / NIEPER** for gravity/tachyon energy as the source;
- not an independent calibrated power balance.

Important genealogy rule: later works that repeat Nieper's source theory are not new Testatika observations.

### 1985 — Nieper publication: earliest currently source-secure printed container for 1984 witness material

`Conversion of Gravity Field Energy / Revolution in Technology, Medicine and Society` (1985) is currently the earliest located, date-secure publication containing a substantial Testatika/Swiss-ML eyewitness record.

It contains at least three distinct layers:
1. Nieper's own account and theory;
2. the reproduced 17-March-1984 `L. L.` witness report;
3. Nieper's relay concerning P. H. Matthey and other DVS inspections.

These layers must be cited separately.

### 1986-02-14 — Albert Hauser visit

Albert Hauser states that he visited Methernitha with two companions for about four hours on **14 February 1986**.

His source genealogy is unusually useful because publication stages are identified:
- short report: `DIFOT-News` no. 5, **May 1986**;
- `UFO-Contact` no. 6, **1986**;
- English version, **February 1987**;
- fuller/revised report: `diföt-nyt` no. 14, **1988**, according to Hauser's later provenance account.

High-value technical observations/claims in the surviving Hauser report include:
- wood foundation plate on the large machine;
- plexiglass/acrylic structure;
- stationary perforated/insulating layers;
- horseshoe-magnet assemblies with bifilar windings and insulating/perforated sheets;
- incomplete/uncertain electrical connections;
- several machine sizes and model-dependent differences.

Status:
- **W1** for Hauser's reported observations;
- **HYPOTHESIS** for his inferred circuit functions;
- do not transfer large-machine internals to M2.

Sources:
- https://www.robkalmeijer.nl/techniek/experiments/testakica/index.html
- https://equapio.com/energie/testatika-legendaere-energiemaschine-der-methernitha/

Acquisition targets: scans of original `DIFOT-News` 5/1986, `UFO-Contact` 6/1986 and `diföt-nyt` 14/1988.

### 1988-02-09 — direct Methernitha institutional letter, signed Francis Bosshard

Stefan Marinov's contemporaneous 1988 `The Thorny Way of Truth, Part III` preserves a Methernitha letter:
- location `CH-3517 Linden`;
- date **9.2.1988**;
- signed **Francis Bosshard** for Methernitha.

The letter's content is primarily ethical/secrecy policy: Methernitha says that simply releasing a `free-energy device` would not solve mankind's deeper problems and invites a disagreeing reader to discover and publish a device independently.

Status:
- **SOURCE-STATED / INSTITUTIONAL PRIMARY preserved in contemporaneous publication**;
- strong evidence for Methernitha's public rationale for withholding the technology;
- **no mechanism or energy-reservoir explanation** is supplied by this letter.

Source:
- Stefan Marinov, `The Thorny Way of Truth, Part III`, second edition 1988; preserved scan/mirror.

### 1988-06 — Stefan Marinov first-person visit note: crucial epistemic constraint

Immediately after the Bosshard material, Marinov writes that in **June 1988** he was invited to Methernitha and spent two days there. He explicitly says that he **did not understand the principle of action and was unable to reproduce it**.

Status:
- **H1 / DIRECT-AUTHOR** for Marinov's own visit and stated lack of understanding;
- this is a hard constraint against later claims that Marinov had already recovered the secret circuit or energy source in 1988.

Source:
- same Marinov TWT-III 1988 scan/mirror.

### 1989 — Marinov, `raum & zeit` issue 40 and TWT-V/VII

The official `raum & zeit` archive confirms Stefan Marinov's `Methernitha und Testatika` in issue 40, 1989, and says his report was published unchanged; two Don Kelly reports follow it.

In his 1989 Testatika writing Marinov makes several source-critical admissions:
- he did not personally dismantle the machine;
- he did not possess its technical drawings;
- drawings available to him came from people such as Albert Hauser or from photographic reconstruction such as Kelly;
- he discusses ordinary terrestrial electric-field collection as a known electrostatic possibility but notes that Testatika lacks the long antenna/baseline such a simple explanation would require.

Status:
- **H1 / MARINOV direct author** for what he did/did not inspect;
- Marinov's own conservation-law/source conclusions remain **HYPOTHESIS**, not operator disclosure.

Sources:
- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/methernitha-und-testatika/
- Marinov TWT-V/TWT-VII preserved scans/mirrors.

### 1991-01/02 — Stefan Hartmann early direct-author mirror

A preserved early Hartmann text is dated **20 January 1991**, with a related message dated **3 February 1991**. Hartmann explicitly says that he personally visited Methernitha.

His technical frame at this stage is important historically:
- Testatika = electrostatic/Wimshurst-like machine plus magnetic additions;
- he proposes that the low electrostatic output is somehow amplified by the magnetic unit;
- he proposes charged/ionized air as the source and also speculates about background/vacuum energy;
- he gives voltage/current/output claims and says he believes the machine is genuine.

Status:
- **H1-M / DIRECT-AUTHOR MIRROR** for `Hartmann says he visited` and for Hartmann's own 1991 theory;
- **HYPOTHESIS / Hartmann** for air/vacuum/magnetic amplification;
- low evidential weight for claimed net output because no closed measurement is supplied.

### 1999-08 — Hans Holzherr direct correspondence

Holzherr's 1999 correspondence is one of the strongest late pre-2000 witness sources because the roles are explicit: **Holzherr observed; Hartmann asked questions/translated/distributed.**

## Source genealogy: do not double-count

The most important duplication chains are:

- `L.L. 17-Mar-1984 report -> Nieper 1985 -> Kelly/later books -> web mirrors`;
- `Hauser 14-Feb-1986 visit -> DIFOT-News 5/1986 -> UFO-Contact 6/1986 / 1987 English -> fuller diföt-nyt 14/1988 -> later mirrors`;
- `Kelly Magnets Aug-1987 -> raum & zeit 40/1989 reprint -> Kelly/Bailey 1991 synthesis -> later manuals/web drawings`;
- `Methernitha 9-Feb-1988 letter -> Marinov TWT-III 1988 -> later quotations`;
- `Methernitha institutional 1989 sound track -> SAFE proceedings -> later translations/summaries`;
- `Marinov 1988/1989 visits -> TWT / raum & zeit / later reprints`;
- `Schneider/Weber 13-Mar-1984 visit -> detailed known 1994 retrospective account -> later books/sites`;
- `Holzherr 1999 correspondence -> multiple Internet mirrors`.

A copied diagram, translated transcript or re-encoded file does not become an additional witness.

## Mechanism clues that survive source-genealogy filtering

### A. Magnetic timing/resonance is an early witness clue, not a late Internet invention

The 17-March-1984 L.L. report already uses the combination:

`~60 rpm + magnetic impulses + strong horseshoe magnets + electrical resonance circuits`.

That materially raises the historical priority of **magnetic timing / resonant conditioning** for the relevant large/two-disc family. It does **not** prove that magnets are the bulk energy source, and it does not transfer automatically to M2.

### B. Layered perforated structures and magnet assemblies recur in Hauser's 1986 observation line

Hauser's large-machine report independently emphasizes perforated/insulating layers, horseshoe magnet assemblies and complex stationary structures. This converges structurally with the L.L. report while still leaving the source of real power unresolved.

### C. The strongest early institutional source explains secrecy, not physics

The 9-Feb-1988 Bosshard/Methernitha letter directly supports the withholding rationale but gives no energy-reservoir mechanism. Therefore later claims that Methernitha officially disclosed `tachyon`, `vacuum`, `earth rotation`, `radium`, etc. need their own operator source.

### D. Marinov explicitly did not have the answer in 1988

Marinov's statement that he did not understand the principle and could not reproduce it is one of the most important controls in the entire corpus. Any later source claiming `Marinov knew the secret` must overcome this dated first-person statement.

### E. The 1989 institutional sound-track transcript is a top acquisition target

Because it is Methernitha's own self-description, the full original proceedings version can distinguish operator language from later Kelly/Marinov/Internet engineering interpretations.

### F. Hartmann's 1991 environmental-air idea is historically early but remains Hartmann's theory

Hartmann's 1991 text is important because environmental electrical coupling was already being considered before the later Internet reconstruction era. But its `charged/ionized air` and possible background/vacuum source statements are **Hartmann hypotheses**, not evidence that Baumann disclosed that mechanism.

### G. Holzherr's mesh-versus-foil Principle Experiment is a high-value operator-linked discriminator

The 1999 report that Baumann said solid foil would not produce the observed effect makes `perforated mesh versus geometrically matched solid foil` one of the strongest low-energy A/B experiments for the reconstruction program. A positive difference would establish field/charge-transport sensitivity, not free energy.

## Evolution of proposed energy-source explanations before 2000

The pre-2000 sources do **not** converge on one disclosed reservoir:

- **Nieper:** gravity-stressing/tachyon field — Nieper theory;
- **L.L. 1984 witness:** magnets/resonance describe charging/timing, but no quantitatively identified bulk reservoir;
- **Hauser:** detailed machine topology, no closed source identification in the early report;
- **Methernitha/Bosshard 1988:** uses `free-energy device` language but withholds mechanism;
- **Marinov 1988/1989:** explicitly does not understand/reproduce the mechanism, then develops his own interpretations;
- **Hartmann 1991:** charged/ionized air and possible background/vacuum source — Hartmann theory;
- **Kelly:** progressively more elaborate electrostatic/magnetic circuit interpretation, largely synthetic;
- **Holzherr 1999:** strong geometry/behavioral clues, no closed source measurement.

Therefore the pre-2000 record supports a historically persistent combination of **electrostatic influence + magnetic timing/bias + resonance/conditioning + storage**, but the **bulk real-power reservoir remains UNKNOWN**.
