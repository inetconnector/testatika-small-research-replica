# Don Kelly source correction — 2026-08-17

## Purpose

This note separates **Donald A. (Don) Kelly** from **Patrick J. Kelly** and records a source-level self-correction by Don Kelly that materially changes the evidentiary value of early Testatika diagrams.

This is a provenance correction, not a mechanism claim.

## 1. Do not conflate the Kelly authors

Three names in the Testatika/free-energy literature are easy to collapse accidentally:

- **Donald A. / Don Kelly** — author of *The Manual of Free Energy Devices and Systems*; Testatika/Swiss M-L author in the 1980s; co-author with Patrick G. Bailey at IECEC 1991; later associated with the Space Energy Association.
- **Patrick J. Kelly** — later author/compiler of *A Practical Guide to 'Free-Energy' Devices*. He is a different person and is not the Kelly in the 1991 Testatika IECEC paper.
- **Patrick G. Bailey** — Don Kelly's co-author on the 1991 IECEC Testatika paper; also a different person from Patrick J. Kelly.

Repository rule: write the first occurrence as `Donald A. (Don) Kelly`, `Patrick J. Kelly`, or `Patrick G. Bailey`; do not use bare `Kelly` where more than one of these people could be meant.

## 2. Don Kelly's 1986 manual already contains the Swiss M-L Converter

Public mirrors of Donald A. Kelly's *The Manual of Free Energy Devices and Systems* identify:

- copyright / first printing: **1986**;
- second printing: **1987**;
- third printing: **1991**;
- ISBN: **0-932298-59-5**.

The Swiss M-L section presents a reconstruction/analysis with, among other things:

- two counter-rotating electrostatic discs;
- metallic sectors;
- a `crystal diode` interpretation;
- collection brushes;
- two horseshoe magnets with coils;
- a hollow cylindrical magnet;
- Leyden-jar/capacitor stages;
- speculative links to Searl/Ecklin/Poggendorff and gravity/tachyon ideas.

Source examples:

- https://pdfcoffee.com/kelly-manual-of-free-energy-devices-pdf-free.html
- https://www.scribd.com/document/835145364/The-Manual-of-Free-Energy-Devices-and-Systems-1-20-1

### Evidence classification

`P0-PUBLICATION-MIRROR / S2-I1 for historical machine construction`

The publication proves what Don Kelly was publishing by 1986. It does **not** prove that he directly inspected the machine. Marinov's direct-author material and Kelly/Bailey's own 1991 paper instead constrain Kelly to indirect evidence such as photographs, visitor documents and video.

## 3. August 1987 Magnets article is independently anchored

The current official `raum & zeit` archive for issue 40/1989 states that Don Kelly described Testatika in **Magnets in August 1987** and made an **electro-schematic diagram**, which `raum & zeit` reproduced in the original.

Source:

- https://www.raum-und-zeit.com/produkt/der-schweizer-ml-konverter/

This is stronger bibliographic evidence for an August-1987 *Magnets* publication than undated later web compilations.

The original *Magnets* issue/pages still need acquisition before exact wording, captions and diagram provenance are treated as locked.

## 4. Don Kelly's 1998 self-correction is a major downgrade of early geometry

In a letter published in *New Energy News*, Vol. 6, No. 4, August 1998, pp. 17–18, Don Kelly discusses Testatika misinformation. He identifies the depiction of the twin electrostatic discs together with nearby horseshoe magnets and windings as having come from an **early study in `Magnets`**, which he dates retrospectively as roughly **1984**. He then says those early-study details were **later shown to be incorrect**.

Primary publication mirror:

- https://www.padrak.com/ine/NEN_6_4_12.html

### Consequence

Any Kelly-derived schematic or reconstruction that places the twin horseshoe magnets/windings at the discs in the manner of that early study is **author-retrospectively disavowed** and must not be used as an original-machine CAD constraint.

This is stronger than a generic warning that Kelly was speculative: it is Don Kelly correcting his own source lineage.

## 5. The `1984` versus `August 1987` date conflict must remain open

Two source statements now coexist:

1. Don Kelly, writing in 1998, refers to an early *Magnets* study as **`(1984) ... approximate date`**.
2. The official `raum & zeit` archive says Kelly's *Magnets* Testatika description and electro-schematic diagram appeared in **August 1987**.

Do **not** silently normalize these to one date.

Possible explanations to test by acquisition:

- there were two distinct *Magnets* publications/studies;
- an underlying study originated around 1984 but was published in 1987;
- Kelly misremembered the date in 1998;
- `raum & zeit` is referring to a later revision rather than the first diagram.

Until the physical/scanned *Magnets* issues are found, status is `DATE/IDENTITY CONFLICT`.

- **More About the Swiss M-L Converter: Pros and Cons**
- *Magnets Magazine*

The 1991 paper itself is:

- Donald A. Kelly and Patrick G. Bailey

- 26th Intersociety Energy Conversion Engineering Conference
- Vol. 4, pp. 467–472
- 1991

As already recorded in the Internet audit, the 1991 authors explicitly expose their evidence boundary: visitor documents/video rather than direct machine inspection, photo-estimated geometry, unsuccessful duplication attempts known to them, and conjectural component interpretation.

## 7. CAD and evidence-matrix rules after this correction

For all current/future Testatika CAD work:

1. **Do not** import the Kelly horseshoe-magnet/coil placement from the early diagram as observed original geometry.
2. Tag Kelly diagrams `S2/I1`, with `PHOTO/SECONDARY-DERIVED` where applicable.
3. If a Kelly geometry item conflicts with Marinov, Hauser, an authenticated video frame or another direct-witness source, the direct source wins unless machine identity differs.
4. Do not infer rubbing `collection brushes` for M2 merely because Kelly's manual says so; direct small-machine sources report non-contact/floating features and must be kept separate.
5. Preserve the possibility that Kelly's various diagrams refer to different machine generations, but require an explicit source bridge before assigning them to M2/M5/M6a.
6. The 1984/1987 *Magnets* date conflict is an acquisition problem, not a CAD parameter.

## Bottom line

The early Don Kelly material is useful as a history of evolving reconstruction hypotheses, but it is now demonstrably unsafe as original-machine geometry. Don Kelly himself later stated that a prominent early magnet/coil depiction was incorrect. The repository should therefore keep Don Kelly's drawings as **versioned secondary reconstructions**, never as a hidden original Testatika schematic.
