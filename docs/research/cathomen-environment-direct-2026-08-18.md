## 1. Direct passage: electricity is already `there`, and Cathomen worries about taking too much from nature

Near the end of the workshop discussion, Cathomen worries about what would happen if very large numbers of the converters were sold. The interviewer asks, in substance, why that would matter **if they take it from the air**.

Cathomen's response is materially stronger than a generic later `free energy` interpretation. In the preserved German transcript he says:

- `Die Elektrizität das ist da ...`;
- he then asks what happens if very many devices use it;
- he compares the effect to lightning / a natural electrical process;
- he says that this natural electricity has a role in `die Natur reinigen, Luft reinigen`;
- and later objects that one could cause harm by `von der Natur` taking something away.

The interviewer, not Cathomen, supplies the explicit phrase `wenn sie das aus der Luft nehmen?` and later argues that it is `Freie Energie` that is always available. Cathomen does **not** simply endorse that unlimited-reservoir framing; he pushes back and expresses uncertainty about ecological consequences.

### Evidence classification

**CATHOMEN-DIRECT / conceptual source belief, not engineering proof.**

What is source-supported:

- Cathomen treats the relevant electricity as something already present in nature/environment rather than merely as energy generated inside a conventional closed machine;
- he believes large-scale extraction might perturb a natural electrical process;
- he accepts the lightning analogy in this discussion.

What is *not* source-supported by this passage alone:

- that ordinary fair-weather atmospheric ion current supplies the claimed watts/kilowatts;
- that the machine literally harvests only air ions;
- the spatial source port (air, ground, atmosphere-ionosphere, building capacitance, RF, etc.);
- any quantitative energy balance;
- any new-physics mechanism.

## 2. Why this passage matters when combined with the other workshop statements

The same transcript separately records:

- energy taken at two poles and sent upward;
- an upper stage where it is `verstärkt`;
- a very-high-voltage state, transcribed around `100 kV` after interviewer clarification;
- the upper device described as increasing `Kapazität`, with magnets and likely coils, while details are withheld;
- energy then returned downward to condensers / Leyden jars and stored;
- non-contact `Abnehmer`, explicitly answered `Ja` when asked whether they are also magnetized;
- a condenser-associated component for which Cathomen says `Vakuum ist es noch nicht`;
- an impulse `Fühler` to keep frequency constant;
- the early development problem of getting only one voltage / `Klapf` and then having no voltage left.

Taken together, the source-compatible workshop architecture is:

`environmental/natural electrical state (operator belief)`

→ `electrostatic influence / rotating variable geometry`

→ `magnetically biased non-contact pickup`

→ `upper conditioning / strengthening stage`

→ `nonlinear / possibly reduced-pressure commutation`

→ `Leyden storage`.

This remains a **hypothesis architecture**, not a recovered schematic.

## 4. Quantitative falsification boundary

Any environmental-source hypothesis must close the energy ledger. For a claimed 100 W output, an equivalent high-voltage source port at 100 kV would need an average current of approximately:

`I = P / V = 100 W / 100 kV = 1 mA`.

For 300 W at 100 kV it would be approximately `3 mA`.

These are target-equivalent power-flow values, not claims that such currents were present historically. Conventional fair-weather atmospheric conduction currents are vastly smaller, so `air/environment` cannot be promoted from operator language to a 100-W mechanism without identifying and measuring a substantially stronger real source port.

## 5. Experimental consequence

Future low-energy mechanism work should distinguish at least these external variables rather than treating `air` as one thing:

- relative humidity and absolute humidity;
- air exchange / ion concentration;
- ambient DC electric field;
- RF/HF electric and magnetic spectra;
- machine-to-earth capacitance;
- front-to-rear differential field/capacitance;
- floating vs grounded rear conductive plate;
- base capacitance / displacement current;
- temperature independently of humidity.

The environmental-source question is resolved only if phase-resolved input power through every identifiable external port is measured against load output and storage changes.
