# Pre-2000 Testatika source excavation — round 3

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Focus:** recovered full 1991 IECEC paper, its explicit source genealogy, the 1989 Methernitha institutional narration, and newly precise pre-1991 bibliographic anchors.  
**Source discipline:** quotation/paraphrase boundaries and author roles are preserved; later reconstructions are not promoted to operator facts.

## 1. Full Kelly/Bailey 1991 IECEC paper recovered

The complete conference paper is publicly retrievable as:

`Donald Kelly; Patrick Bailey, The Methernitha Free Energy Machine — The Swiss M-L Converter, 26th IECEC, Vol. 4, 1991, pp. 467-472.`

Direct PDF:
- https://www.padrak.com/ine/METHERNITHA_IECEC_1991.pdf

Bibliographic confirmation:
- https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=200902001434259167

This matters because the paper explicitly tells us what its authors actually knew and where their claims came from.

### Author epistemic limit

Kelly/Bailey state that their real evidence consisted of:

- documents from researchers who had seen/tested the devices; and
- a videotape showing a machine in operation.

They do **not** claim that the two paper authors themselves dismantled, instrumented, or independently power-balanced an original Methernitha converter.

Evidence consequence:

`Kelly/Bailey 1991 = engineering synthesis + source container`, not a new independent eyewitness for every repeated claim.

## 2. The paper explicitly labels its 1985 material as quotation/paraphrase

The `BACKGROUND` section says it begins **quotations and paraphrasing from Ref. 1**, published in May 1985. Ref. 1 is Nieper's 1985 book, pages 359-362.

Therefore claims in this block must be traced back to Nieper/Matthey/L.L. source layers rather than cited as independent Kelly observations.

The block reproduces the already known early cluster:

- approximately 50-60 rpm;
- electrostatic/influence charging;
- lattice/grid electrodes and Leyden storage;
- magnetic impulse synchronization;
- strong horseshoe magnets described in relation to resonance circuits;
- claimed 300 V / 10 A or 3-4 kW demonstrations.

This confirms source genealogy but adds no independent proof of net power.

## 3. The 17-Mar-1984 magnetic-resonance wording is genuinely upstream of Kelly

The IECEC paper preserves the 1985 source block saying the disc speed is synchronized by magnetic impulses and that strong horseshoe magnets are involved in resonance circuits.

Because Kelly explicitly identifies this section as quotation/paraphrase of the 1985 source, the `magnetic timing + resonance` idea cannot be dismissed as a Kelly-only 1991 invention.

Correct status:

- early witness/source wording: **historically real**;
- exact operator origin of the causal sentence: **unresolved**;
- bulk energy reservoir: **not identified**.

## 4. Hauser 1986/1987 is separately quoted

Kelly/Bailey then explicitly switch source genealogy and say they are quoting Albert Hauser's report after his 1986 visit, from an English translation published in 1987.

The excerpt says, among other things:

- the machine had to be hand-started and was reported to continue running;
- a large machine was tested with a nominal 1000-W lamp;
- a smaller machine was run for about two hours but tested only with measuring instruments, with Hauser estimating roughly 200 W.

This confirms that the large-machine lamp demonstration and the smaller-machine no-load/instrument test are separate observations.

It also strengthens the acquisition target for the original 1987 English Hauser publication rather than relying on later web copies.

## 5. Kelly's own 1991 reconstruction is explicitly conjectural

After the quoted source material, the paper says a description was **formulated based upon the information available**. It repeatedly uses terms equivalent to `thought to be`, `surmised`, `may`, and `not all understood` for the internal magnetic/crystal/flux-enhancement stage.

The authors hypothesize:

- a modified Wimshurst electrostatic front end;
- non-contacting driving/collecting electrodes;
- Leyden-jar/capacitive stages;
- a magnetic/crystal `solid state flux enhancement` stage;
- resonant coupling that might control disc speed;
- possible current amplification / electron-avalanche behavior.

These are **KELLY/BAILEY HYPOTHESES**, not Methernitha circuit disclosure.

This source distinction is important because many later websites reproduce the Kelly diagram as though it were the actual hidden Testatika schematic.

## 6. The paper contains an unusually valuable skeptical section

Kelly/Bailey include a section titled `SOLICITED OPPOSING VIEWS`. It explicitly raises conventional objections:

- electrostatic influence machines do not ordinarily produce the claimed power;
- a claimed crystal/permanent-magnet rectification function is technically suspicious;
- Poggendorff electrostatic-motor behavior does not by itself imply a self-running loaded generator;
- incandescent-lamp/heater demonstrations are poor average-power metrology in high-voltage/high-frequency conditions;
- magnets can transform voltage/current relationships but do not by themselves establish net power gain;
- tests away from buildings were not documented in the source set available to the authors.

This is a major provenance correction: the 1991 paper is **not** simply an uncritical over-unity endorsement. It carries both the proponent reconstruction and explicit measurement/source objections.

## 8. The 1989 Methernitha institutional soundtrack is directly quoted in the IECEC paper

Reference 10 is explicitly:

`Methernitha, Informationsfilm Thesta-Distatica: Sound Track Transcription, Proceedings of the International Congress for Free Energy, Einsiedeln, Switzerland, 1989, ISBN 3-9520025-1-8.`

Kelly/Bailey then mark a section as beginning a quotation from the **Methernitha Informationsfilm English narration**.

The quoted institutional text says, in substance:

- Methernitha seeks technologies that use forces inherent in nature without damaging ecological balance;
- nature is described as the greatest source of power and knowledge;
- the device is presented as something learned from nature rather than as an ordinary fuel-driven machine;
- conventional physics terminology is said to be only partly adequate for describing it;
- the broader passage is strongly ethical/spiritual rather than a disclosure of the missing energy-coupling circuit.

Evidence class:
- **SOURCE-STATED / institutional**, preserved in a 1991 proceedings paper that directly cites the 1989 proceedings source;
- high value for Methernitha's own conceptual framing;
- still not a quantitative identification of the external real-power reservoir.

## 9. Technical official-video wording: source lineage narrowed but date must stay careful

A surviving Rimstar archive says its clips come from a promotional video assembled by the Methernitha community and provides an English translation of the technical narration. It includes the familiar institutional sequence:

- counterrotating discs generate electrostatic charge;
- one disc represents earth, the other cloud;
- grid electrodes bind charge;
- non-contact `antenna keys` collect/sort it;
- initial hand rotation is followed by electrostatic attraction/repulsion cycles;
- a rectifying diode is said to stabilize those cycles;
- slow, steady speed is important;
- grid condensers store/smooth energy and additional devices reduce voltage/build useful power;
- output is described as DC and humidity-sensitive.

Source:
- https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/videos.htm
- https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/methernitha_testatika_technical_info.htm

The wording strongly overlaps with the Methernitha text quoted from the 1989 Informationsfilm in the IECEC paper. However, until the full original 1989 transcript is acquired, the **technical paragraph itself should not be back-dated line-for-line to 1989 solely from the later web archive**.

Safe rule:

- `nature / ecological / terminology` wording = directly tied to 1989 narration through IECEC;
- `earth/cloud + antenna keys + rectifying diode + grid condensers` = official Methernitha video/website lineage, strongly related but exact earliest printed date still under acquisition.

## 10. A new hard source-boundary from the IECEC paper

The 1991 authors say that the lack of detailed technical information prevents direct classification of the device. That statement is especially important because the same paper later proposes detailed internal functions.

Therefore:

`paper schematic != recovered Methernitha schematic`.

Any reconstruction should mark Kelly's internal component functions as **HYPOTHESIS** unless corroborated by a closer source such as Marinov direct correspondence, Hauser direct observation, Holzherr, Cathomen, or institutional wording.

## 11. Implication for the current energy-source search

The oldest source chain now separates three different questions cleanly:

1. **What produces/organizes charge?**  
   Early sources repeatedly point to electrostatic influence, grid structures, magnetic impulse timing and resonance-related components.

2. **What keeps timing/rotation stable?**  
   Early witness and later institutional wording point to magnetic impulses / slow steady speed / rectifying or gating functions, depending on machine family and source.

3. **Where does the sustained real power come from?**  
   Still unresolved. `gravity stressing`, `vacuum`, `charged air`, and `flux enhancement` are theorist labels, not a single convergent operator-disclosed reservoir.

This makes the best historical working chain:

`unknown environmental/bulk reservoir -> electrostatic charge state -> magnetic/resonant timing/conditioning -> nonlinear/gating stage -> Leyden/grid storage -> load`

with the first arrow still **UNKNOWN**.
