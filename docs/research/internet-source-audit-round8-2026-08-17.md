# Internet source audit — round 8 — 2026-08-17

## Scope

Round 8 focuses on **source dependence** in the early large-machine literature. A repeated claim is not independent evidence when a later author is reusing an earlier witness/report bundle. This round materially changes how Kelly/Nieper/GAGFE claims are counted, but does not alter CAD geometry by itself.

## 1. Nieper identifies the source bundle behind the “five inspections” claim

Public mirror of Hans A. Nieper's expanded book:

- https://www.scribd.com/document/131614540/33883875-Nieper-Conversion-of-Gravity-Field-Energy-Revolution-in-Technology-Medicine-and-Society-1985

In the section `The Swiss ML-Converter — A Masterpiece of Craftsmanship and Electronic Engineering`, Nieper states that members of the German Association of Gravity Field Energy (`DVS`, often rendered GAGFE in English sources) inspected the converter on **five different occasions**.

He then names two concrete visits:

- Swiss electronics engineer **P. H. Matthey**, a DVS member: **20 October 1984**, with several other Swiss engineers;
- **Hans A. Nieper himself**: **28 October 1984**.

Nieper also reproduces a short Matthey statement from Matthey's report: Matthey judged the absence of a primary propulsion source as not explainable by conventional teaching physics. The original Matthey report is still not acquired, so this remains `MATTHEY → NIEPER PUBLICATION`, not a direct scan of Matthey's report.

## 2. Nieper's five-visit statement is not five independently documented tests

The book gives the aggregate count `five different occasions`, but does **not** in the located passage identify all five dates, observer lists, instrumentation or test protocols.

Later, after reproducing the 17-March-1984 L.L. report, Nieper says selected DVS members also inspected the converter in 1983 and 1984.

Therefore the correct evidence representation is:

- existence of a DVS/GAGFE multi-visit **claim** = source-stated by Nieper;
- Matthey 20-Oct-1984 = named visit line;
- Nieper 28-Oct-1984 = named first-person visit line;
- L.L. 17-Mar-1984 = separately reproduced witness report;
- remaining visits = **UNRESOLVED**, not five independent confirmations.

No confidence multiplication is allowed merely from the number `five`.

## 3. Kelly's “G.A.G.F.E. five visits” text is downstream, not independent

Don Kelly's `Manual of Free Energy Devices and Systems` contains a Testatika/Swiss M-L section which says members of the G.A.G.F.E. inspected the Swiss system on five occasions from 1984 onward.

Public text mirror:

- https://www.scribd.com/doc/7954244/The-Manual-of-Free-Energy-Devices-and-Systems-1991

The structural and verbal overlap with Nieper is substantial:

- same title phrase `A Masterpiece of Craftsmanship and Electronic Engineering`;
- same five-inspection organizational claim;
- same operation-since-1982 line;
- same 3–4 kW / 230 V DC figure;
- same no-primary-propulsion framing;
- same ball-bearing wear detail;
- same acrylic / light-metal lattice / insulated copper / crystal-diode / gold-contact component list.

Kelly then continues with geometry/demo details that closely track the **L.L. 17-March-1984 report reproduced by Nieper**, including:

- ~50 fan-like sectors;
- ~60 rpm;
- magnetic synchronization;
- central rainbow-coloured disc;
- hand startup;
- Leyden-jar readiness;
- lamp/heater demonstration.

### Decision

For overlapping facts:

`Nieper/L.L./Matthey transmission → Kelly manual`

is the default source-dependence model unless Kelly provides an independently identified witness or document.

Thus the Kelly manual **must not be counted as an independent witness confirmation** of these geometry/output details.

## 4. Kelly's own 1991 IECEC paper supports this downgrade

Round 2 already located the Kelly/Bailey 1991 IECEC paper. The authors explicitly state that their real evidence consisted of documents from researchers who had seen/tested devices plus a videotape, and that known replication attempts had been unsuccessful.

This is consistent with the source-dependence conclusion above: Kelly was primarily compiling and interpreting witness/document/video material rather than reporting his own machine inspection.

## 5. Important separation: Kelly theory versus inherited witness geometry

Kelly's manual adds a theoretical analysis involving:

- Wimshurst analogy;
- `Searl effect`;
- `Ecklin principle`;
- Poggendorff motor effect;
- three proposed electrical circuits;
- crystal-capacitance / frequency-regulation interpretation;
- horseshoe-magnet coil interpretation.

These are **Kelly analysis/hypotheses** unless separately supported by an earlier direct machine source.

The inherited witness geometry and Kelly's theoretical overlay must therefore be stored as two different evidence layers:

1. `DOWNSTREAM WITNESS-DERIVED DESCRIPTION`;
2. `KELLY MECHANISM INTERPRETATION`.

The latter cannot be promoted because it happens to sit next to inherited direct-witness facts in the same publication.

## 6. Kelly's later 1998 self-correction reinforces the versioning requirement

Round 6 established that Kelly himself later said an early `Magnets` depiction with two horseshoe magnets/windings close to the twin discs had been shown incorrect.

Therefore Kelly material must be **versioned by date**:

- early `Magnets` reconstruction: some geometry later retracted;
- manual-era reconstruction: mixes inherited witness facts and Kelly theory;
- 1991 IECEC: explicitly discloses indirect evidence boundary and competing views;
- 1998 letter: explicitly corrects one influential early depiction.

There is no timeless single object called “the Kelly schematic”.

## 7. L.L. report provenance strengthened but identity still unresolved

Nieper's book explicitly ends the 17-March-1984 report with:

`L. L., Rorschach, Switzerland`.

The report describes the observed large machine in detail and is therefore a valuable witness publication. However:

- full identity of `L. L.` is still unresolved;
- original typed report/manuscript is not acquired;
- strong textual/geometry similarity to the Schneider/Weber 13-March-1984 line remains unresolved for source independence;
- its energy-source explanations and `tachyon` framing are interpretation, not measured mechanism.

M5 and M5a remain separate-but-linked until provenance proves identity/dependence.

## 8. Matthey line becomes more precise

Current strongest chain:

- Nieper says P. H. Matthey, DVS member and Swiss electronics engineer, visited on **20-Oct-1984** with several Swiss engineers;
- Nieper quotes one conclusion from Matthey's report;
- the original Matthey report is missing;
- a later Pierre M. Matthey `raum&zeit` article shows a more philosophical/machine-skeptical priority, but does not directly retract the 1984 observation.

Therefore:

`Matthey visit = SOURCE-STATED BY NIEPER / named near-primary lead`

not yet `DIRECT ORIGINAL REPORT`.

## 9. Greenhouse claim remains unverified as an energy-balance fact

Nieper says the machine, possibly together with one or two others, had been operating since 1982 and among other things heating a roughly 5,500-square-foot greenhouse at roughly 2,600-foot altitude in winter.

This is historically interesting but currently lacks:

- meter records;
- connection diagram;
- duration logs;
- input/storage accounting;
- independent facility documentation.

It is therefore retained only as a **Nieper source-stated operational claim**, not as proof of net energy.

## 10. New source-independence rule

For every future repeated Testatika fact, ask:

1. Is the later author actually a witness?
2. Does the later text identify a different witness/document?
3. Is wording/sequence suspiciously similar to an earlier publication?
4. Are dimensions and demo details copied as a block?
5. Did the later author explicitly disclose indirect evidence?

If the answer indicates reuse, confidence does **not** increase from repetition.

## 12. CAD consequence

**No immediate CAD change.**

The current Hauser M6a line remains the best direct construction anchor because it contains machine-specific visit/reconstruction material and later detailed internal-cylinder observations. Round 8 mainly reduces the apparent independence of Kelly/Nieper-derived large-machine details.

Any future CAD change based only on Kelly must state whether the feature is:

- inherited witness description;
- Kelly photo-fit;
- Kelly mechanism hypothesis;
- or later-corrected Kelly geometry.
