# Sources and provenance

## Stefan Marinov — *The Thorny Way of Truth, Part V* (1989)

This is the primary published source used for the V2 small-machine reconstruction. Marinov reports that:
- he observed/tested a small machine with an approximately 20 cm disc;
- the first small machine is the right-hand machine in figures 13/14;
- early radial sectors were simple copper wires;
- there were no rubbing collection brushes;
- horseshoe magnets were visible on the first small machine;
- the second small machine did not visibly require those magnets;
- the precise way wires passed through the disc mattered;
- he did not know the full operating secret or exact schematic.

Internet Archive identifier used during research:
`thornywayoftruthpart5maririch`

## Albert Hauser material
Hauser is important for larger machines and the non-contact electrode principle. Larger-machine details are not automatically transferred to the small Marinov machine.

## Potter / Kelly / later reconstructions
These are treated as hypothesis catalogs and comparative material, not as proof of original small-machine construction.
