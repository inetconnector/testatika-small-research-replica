# Sources and provenance

## Stefan Marinov — *The Thorny Way of Truth, Part V* (1989)

This is the primary published source used for the V2 small-machine reconstruction. Marinov reports that:
- he observed/tested a small machine with an approximately 20 cm disc;
- the first small machine is the right-hand machine in figures 13/14;
- early radial sectors were simple copper wires;
- there were no rubbing collection brushes;
- horseshoe magnets were visible on the first small machine;
- the second small machine did not visibly require those magnets;
- the precise way wires passed through the disc mattered;
- he did not know the full operating secret or exact schematic.

Internet Archive identifier used during research:
`thornywayoftruthpart5maririch`

### Important wording correction

No primary Marinov passage has yet been verified in which he literally says Baumann's explanation sounded like an "unknown language". What is supported is:

- Marinov did **not understand the complete machine**;
- Hans Holzherr independently reported difficulty understanding Baumann because Baumann spoke softly/quickly and used non-scientific terminology;
- Methernitha's own technical description explicitly says conventional physical terms describe their claimed functions only approximately and uses special vocabulary such as `Taster` / `antenna keys`.

See [`research/baumann-language-decoding.md`](research/baumann-language-decoding.md).

## Albert Hauser material

Hauser is important for larger machines and the non-contact electrode principle. Larger-machine details are not automatically transferred to the small Marinov machine.

## Hans Holzherr — 1999 visit report

Archive:
`https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/report99.htm`

Additional correspondence mirror:
`https://www.novakcorp.com/energy/experiments/tesnews.htm`

Source value:
- direct visitor evidence for the 50 cm machine and Principle Experiment;
- source-stated Baumann remarks such as mesh vs. solid foil and explicit rejection of radium chloride;
- important direct observation that Baumann's technical explanations were given in non-scientific language.

The translation date 4 August 1999 should not be confused with the demonstration date; the later seminar program dates the engineering visit to 5 June 1999.

## Methernitha institutional technical description

Archived copy:
`https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/methernitha_testatika_technical_info.htm`

This is a key source for **Methernitha's vocabulary**, including:
- earth/cloud analogy;
- grid electrodes;
- non-contact `Taster` / `antenna keys`;
- sorting of charges;
- rectifying diode keeping cycles steady;
- slow and steady speed;
- grid capacitors;
- DC output;
- humidity dependence.

It is an operator/institutional explanation, not independent validation of the claimed energy output.

## Principle Experiment

Direct visitor account within:
`https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/report99.htm`

Important source-stated detail:
- Baumann said substituting solid metal foil for wire mesh would not produce the same effect.

This is treated as a high-priority geometry/field experiment, not as an over-unity proof.

## Linden Experiment — second hand

`https://rimstar.org/sdenergy/testa/lindenexp.htm`

The page explicitly warns that the description is second hand and from memory. It attributes to Baumann a principle explanation involving very fine random/fluctuating particles and the need to `rectify` their flux, followed by a horseshoe-magnet/capacitor demonstration.

Status: **H2**. It is important for decoding Baumann's conceptual language but is not a verified original schematic or energy measurement.

## Luzi Cathomen amateur-video transcript

Transcript mirror:
`https://www.allmystery.de/themen/gw11473-5`

Speaker identification matters: this is **Luzi Cathomen**, not Paul Baumann.

Useful workshop statements include:
- special-alloy segments;
- non-contact pickups;
- Leyden-jar storage;
- very high voltage before downstream conditioning;
- an upper capacity/conditioning stage;
- impulse/speed synchronization sensor;
- the historical development problem of keeping electrostatic voltage continuously available rather than obtaining one isolated discharge.

Treat this as a direct workshop/operator source but keep it separate from Baumann quotations.

## Mike Watson recollection

`https://www.novakcorp.com/energy/experiments/bswiss.htm`

Transmission chain: Marinov → Watson → email/web. Therefore H2.

Potentially useful clues:
- iron-wire vs. copper-wire conflict;
- east-west orientation claim;
- shield-plate observation;
- large-machine Fe-Ni sector material.

Marinov's own published observations have priority when they conflict.

## Late Alexander Frolov compilation

`https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356969854_New_Sources_of_Energy_English_version`

Useful only as a lead to older sources for claims attributed to Baumann, including:
- do not withdraw electrons directly from the discs;
- `random particles` / `straightening` terminology;
- alleged 1 kOhm inter-sector connections;
- electricity from air.

Because the original provenance is not established in this repository, these remain H2/I1 and are not baseline construction facts.

## Potter / Kelly / later reconstructions

These are treated as hypothesis catalogs and comparative material, not as proof of original small-machine construction.

## Conventional physics comparison sources

### Heinrich Wommelsdorf — US883846A

`https://patents.google.com/patent/US883846A/en`

Historical primary patent for multiple influence/condenser machines with oppositely rotating systems and embedded/insulated sectors. It demonstrates that several visually unusual Testatika motifs have conventional electrostatic precedents.

### PMMA charge storage and humidity

DOI: `10.1016/S0304-3886(98)00023-0`

### Grid geometry and corona

DOI: `10.1016/j.elstat.2019.103367`

### Humidity in a point-grid-plane corona system

DOI: `10.1016/0304-3886(95)00009-Y`

These papers are used to interpret humidity, grid geometry, surface charge and corona behavior. They do not validate a net-energy anomaly.

## Stefan Hartmann / Overunity.com archival trail — V6

A dedicated source audit is maintained in [`research/hartmann-overunity-testatika.md`](research/hartmann-overunity-testatika.md) with a machine-readable ledger in [`research/hartmann-overunity-sources.tsv`](research/hartmann-overunity-sources.tsv).

Key provenance findings:

- **1992:** Stefan Hartmann directly compared the Testatika with William Hyde's electrostatic rotor/stator patent and referenced an earlier Testatika animation: `https://groups.google.com/g/sci.energy/c/pVGecAtRSXc`.
- **1999:** Hans Holzherr's eyewitness email is distinct from Hartmann's role as questioner/translator/distributor. The correspondence explicitly records Hartmann asking permission to put Holzherr's picture on the `overunity.com` server: `https://www.novakcorp.com/energy/experiments/tesnews.htm`.
- **June 2000:** a historical mirror preserves a Hartmann email proposing an electret/influence interpretation with non-contact `Taster` pickups and capacitive voltage transformation: `https://www.robkalmeijer.nl/techniek/experiments/testakica/index.html`.
- **Later:** the same mirror preserves a materially different Hartmann hypothesis involving weak radioactive minerals and beta electrons. This is a later Hartmann hypothesis, not a Baumann statement.
- **2008:** an interview records Hartmann associating Testatika with `negative resistance` and excited radioactive decay: `https://dandelionsalad.wordpress.com/2008/06/27/free-energy-and-the-open-source-energy-movement-part-one/`.
- Historical external links preserve an Overunity Testatika `topic 75` and media paths `testa01.rm`, `testa02.rm`, `meth5.asf`.
- The current `overunityarchives.com` search page indicates full archive access is restricted to paid subscribers; therefore V6 does not claim exhaustive post-by-post coverage of the old forum.

**Source rule:** Hartmann is treated as an important archivist/distributor and evolving hypothesis author, not as a stable primary witness to the internal Testatika circuit. A Hartmann-hosted Holzherr statement remains a Holzherr statement.
